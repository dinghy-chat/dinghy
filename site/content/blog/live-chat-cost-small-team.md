# What a 3-person team pays for live chat in 2026

A pricing page shows you the price on the day you sign up, for the team you are today. The bill a year later is often a different number.

So we made up a team and followed its bill for twelve months. Three people, one website, about 80 chats a month to start. Prices come from each vendor's own pricing page on November 8 and 9, 2026. Where a vendor shows two prices, like yearly and monthly, we give both.

## Month one

| Product | Per month |
|---|---|
| tawk.to | $0, with "Powered by tawk.to" on the chat |
| Dinghy, self-hosted | $0, plus the server you run it on |
| Dinghy Cloud | $7 |
| Tidio Starter | $24.17 |
| Crisp Mini | $45 |
| Chatwoot Startups | $57 |
| LiveChat Essential | $57 to $75 |
| Intercom Essential | $57 to $87 |

If it ended here, tawk.to wins. Crisp and Chatwoot have free plans too, but they stop at two people, and our team has three.

## Month four, someone joins

The team hires a fourth person for support.

On the per-seat tools, the bill goes up the day that person gets a login. Chatwoot goes to $76. LiveChat to $76 to $100. Intercom to $76 to $116.

Crisp Mini already covers four people, so it stays at $45. Tidio Starter includes up to 10 seats, so it stays at $24.17. tawk.to is still free. Dinghy Cloud is still $7, it covers up to five.

## Month six, a busy week

The team launches something, and chats jump to about 300 that month.

Most plans here don't charge by conversation. Tidio does. Starter includes 100 conversations a month, so a month like this pushes the team to Growth, which starts at $49.17.

## Month nine, the badge

The team wants the chat to look like part of its own site. So the other company's badge has to go.

On tawk.to that's $29 a month. On Tidio, removing the branding is a $16.67 add-on, from Growth up. On Crisp, the "We run on Crisp" label stays on every plan below Plus, which is $295 a month. So our team keeps it.

## Month twelve, the AI pitch

By now every vendor has pitched an AI agent. The prices are per answer or per bundle.

Intercom's Fin costs $0.99 per outcome, on top of seats. If Fin resolves 100 chats a month, that's $99 more. Tidio's Lyro starts at $32.50 a month for 50 AI conversations. tawk.to's AI Assist starts at $29 a month. Chatwoot's paid plans come with some Captain AI credits, then it's $20 per 1,000.

Our team says no to all of it, so the totals below leave AI out. Dinghy has no AI of its own and no AI fee. If you want a bot, you connect your own through MCP or the API and pay only your model provider. See [Bring your own AI agent](/blog/bring-your-own-ai-agent).

## A year later

Four people, one busy month behind them, and no badge where they could pay to remove it:

| Product | Month one | Month twelve |
|---|---|---|
| tawk.to | $0 | $29 |
| Dinghy Cloud | $7 | $7 |
| Tidio | $24.17 | $65.84, Growth plus branding removal |
| Crisp | $45 | $45, label still on |
| Chatwoot | $57 | $76 |
| LiveChat | $57 to $75 | $76 to $100 |
| Intercom | $57 to $87 | $76 to $116, before any Fin answers |

Every one of these costs is on the vendor's pricing page. That's how a "$24 a month" tool ends up at three times that.

## Why our line is flat

Dinghy Cloud is $7 a month because it does less. It's a chat on your website and a shared inbox for up to five people. No built-in AI bot, no shared email inbox, no help center, no campaigns. If you need those, pick another tool.

The plan stops at five people. You pay at signup. There's no free plan or trial, and you can cancel anytime. If you'd rather pay nothing, Dinghy is free on [your own server](/blog/self-host-live-chat).
