# What a 3-person team pays for live chat in 2026

A pricing page shows you the price on the day you sign up, for the team you are today. The bill a year later is often a different number.

So we made up a team and followed its bill for twelve months. Three people, one website, about 80 chats a month to start. We checked the prices on November 22, 2026. The [comparison table](/compare/) links to the vendors. Where a price depends on billing or a promotion, we show a range.

## Month one

| Product | Per month |
|---|---|
| tawk.to | $0, with "Powered by tawk.to" on the chat |
| Dinghy, self-hosted | $0, plus the server you run it on |
| Dinghy Cloud | $9 |
| Tidio Starter, Human only | $24 to $29 |
| Crisp Mini | $45 |
| Chatwoot Startups | $57 |
| LiveChat Essential | $57 to $75 |
| Intercom Essential | $57 to $87 |

If it ended here, tawk.to wins. Crisp and Chatwoot have free plans too, but they stop at two people, and our team has three.

## Month four, someone joins

The team hires a fourth person for support.

On the per-seat tools, the bill goes up the day that person gets a login. Chatwoot goes to $76. LiveChat to $76 to $100. Intercom to $76 to $116.

Crisp Mini already covers four people, so it stays at $45. Tidio Starter includes up to 10 seats, so it stays at $24 to $29. tawk.to is still free. Dinghy Cloud is still $9 per workspace for up to five people.

## Month six, a busy week

The team launches something, and chats jump to about 300 that month.

Tidio’s Human only Starter price in the table covers 100 conversations a month. Human only Growth starts at $41 a month billed annually or $49 billed monthly for 200. With 300 chats, check the price for a 300-conversation quota. The starting price does not cover that volume.

## Month nine, the badge

The team wants the chat to look like part of its own site. So the other company's badge has to go.

On tawk.to that's $29 a month. Tidio offers branding removal as a paid add-on. On Crisp, the "We run on Crisp" label stays on every plan below Plus, which is $295 a month. So our team keeps it.

## Month twelve, the AI pitch

The team looks at adding AI. Costs depend on what the vendor charges for, from resolved conversations to bundles of credits.

Intercom's Fin costs $0.99 per outcome, on top of seats. If Fin resolves 100 chats a month, that's $99 more. Tidio’s Starter with Lyro AI starts at $390 billed annually for 50 AI and human conversations a month. tawk.to's AI Assist starts at $29 a month. Chatwoot's paid plans come with some Captain AI credits, then it's $20 per 1,000.

Our team says no to all of it, so the totals below leave AI out. Dinghy has no AI of its own and no AI fee. If you want a bot, you connect your own through MCP or the API and pay only your model provider. See [Bring your own AI agent](/blog/bring-your-own-ai-agent).

## A year later

Four people, one busy month behind them, and no badge where they could pay to remove it:

| Product | Month one | Month twelve |
|---|---|---|
| tawk.to | $0 | $29 |
| Dinghy Cloud | $9 | $9 |
| Tidio | $24 to $29 | Depends on the conversation quota and branding add-on |
| Crisp | $45 | $45, label still on |
| Chatwoot | $57 | $76 |
| LiveChat | $57 to $75 | $76 to $100 |
| Intercom | $57 to $87 | $76 to $116, before any Fin answers |

These are bills for two example months. They leave out taxes and any model bill for a bot you run yourself.

## Why our line is flat

Dinghy Cloud is $9 a month for website chat and a shared inbox for up to five people. [Proactive campaigns](/blog/proactive-live-chat) and site analytics are included. Dinghy has no built-in AI bot, shared email inbox or help center.

The plan stops at five people. You pay at signup. There's no free plan or trial, and you can cancel anytime. If you'd rather pay nothing, Dinghy is free on [your own server](/blog/self-host-live-chat).

Count the seats and add-ons you'll need over the next year. The [comparison table](/compare/) has the same tools for teams of one and five.
