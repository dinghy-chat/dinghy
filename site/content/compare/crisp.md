# Dinghy vs Crisp

Crisp is the product closest to Dinghy. It's priced per workspace instead of per seat, and its free plan covers two people. So this comparison is mostly about what you need once you outgrow free.

## The quick answer

Stay on Crisp Free if you're one or two people, under 100 contacts, and happy with Crisp's branding. Pick Crisp's paid plans if you want email, a knowledge base, AI and lots of integrations. Pick Dinghy if you want a paid, unbranded chat for a small team at $7 a month, or a chat you can run on your own server.

## Price for a small team

| | Dinghy Cloud | Crisp |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 2 people | $7 a month | $0 on Free, with 100 contacts |
| 3 to 4 people | $7 a month | $45 a month on Mini |
| 5 people | $7 a month | $95 a month on Essentials |
| "We run on Crisp" label | No label | Removed only on Plus, $295 a month |
| AI | None built in. Bring your own agent, no AI fee | AI chatbot on higher plans |
| Self-hosting | Free, open source | Not possible |

Crisp prices are from crisp.chat/en/pricing on November 8, 2026. Mini stops at 4 people, so a fifth person means Essentials. [All the prices side by side](/compare/).

## What Crisp does that Dinghy doesn't

- A free plan. Dinghy has none on the cloud. Self-hosting is the free option.
- A shared email inbox, a knowledge base, and more channels. Dinghy only emails your reply to a visitor who left, and they can answer from their mail app.
- A built-in AI chatbot on the higher plans.
- Native mobile apps. Dinghy's operator app is a web app you can install on your phone, with push alerts.
- A marketplace of integrations. Dinghy has an API, webhooks and an MCP server instead.

## What Dinghy does differently

Crisp's paid plans keep its "We run on Crisp" label until the $295 Plus plan, and count your contacts. Dinghy shows no label of its own, doesn't count contacts, and Dinghy Cloud costs $7 a month for up to 5 people, with no per-person add-ons.

Dinghy has no AI of its own and no AI fee. If you want a bot, connect your own agent through MCP or the API. Its replies wait as drafts until a person sends them, unless you let it send directly. See [Bring your own AI agent](/blog/bring-your-own-ai-agent). If you want a hosted bot that learns from your help center with no setup, Crisp is the better fit.
