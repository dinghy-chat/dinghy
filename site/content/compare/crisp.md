# Dinghy vs Crisp

Crisp is the closest tool to Dinghy. It charges per workspace, not per seat, and its free plan covers two people.

## The quick answer

Stay on Crisp Free if you're one or two people, under 100 contacts, and fine with Crisp's branding. Pay for Crisp if you need email, a knowledge base, AI and integrations. Pick Dinghy if you want a paid chat without anyone's branding for a small team at $7 a month, or a chat on your own server.

## Price for a small team

| | Dinghy Cloud | Crisp |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 2 people | $7 a month | $0 on Free, with 100 contacts |
| 3 to 4 people | $7 a month | $45 a month on Mini |
| 5 people | $7 a month | $95 a month on Essentials |
| "We run on Crisp" label | "Powered by Dinghy" turns off for free | Removed only on Plus, $295 a month |
| AI | None built in. Bring your own agent, no AI fee | AI chatbot on higher plans |
| Self-hosting | Free, open source | Not possible |

Crisp prices are from crisp.chat/en/pricing on November 8, 2026. Mini stops at 4 people, so a fifth person means Essentials. [All the prices side by side](/compare/).

## What Crisp does that Dinghy doesn't

- A free plan. Dinghy Cloud doesn't have one. Self-hosting is the free option.
- A shared email inbox, a knowledge base and more channels. Dinghy only emails your reply to a visitor who left, and they can answer from their mail app.
- A built-in AI chatbot on the higher plans.
- Native mobile apps. Dinghy's operator app is a web app you install on your phone, with push alerts.
- A marketplace of integrations. Dinghy has an API, webhooks and an MCP server instead.

## What Dinghy does differently

Crisp's paid plans keep the "We run on Crisp" label until the $295 Plus plan, and they count your contacts. Dinghy's "Powered by" line turns off for free on any plan, and Dinghy doesn't count contacts. Dinghy Cloud is $7 a month for up to 5 people, no per-person add-ons.

Dinghy has no AI of its own, so no AI fee either. If you want a bot, connect your own agent through MCP or the API. Its replies wait as drafts until a person sends them, unless you let it send on its own. See [Bring your own AI agent](/blog/bring-your-own-ai-agent). But if you want a hosted bot that learns from your help center with zero setup, use Crisp.
