# Dinghy vs Crisp

Crisp and Dinghy Cloud charge per workspace. Crisp has a free plan for two people. Dinghy costs $9 a month for up to five.

## Which one fits

Stay on Crisp Free if you're one or two people with under 100 contacts. Pay for Crisp if you need email, a knowledge base, AI and integrations. Pick Dinghy if you want to host it yourself or pay $9 a month for up to five people.

## Price for a small team

| | Dinghy Cloud | Crisp |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 2 people | $9 a month | $0 on Free, with 100 contacts |
| 3 to 4 people | $9 a month | $45 a month on Mini |
| 5 people | $9 a month | $95 a month on Essentials |
| Screenshots and files in chat | Included | From Mini, $45 a month |
| Webhooks | Included | From Essentials, $95 a month |
| AI | None built in. Bring your own agent, no AI fee | AI chatbot on higher plans |
| Self-hosting | Free, open source | Not possible |

Crisp prices are from [Crisp’s pricing page](https://crisp.chat/en/pricing/). Mini stops at 4 people, so a fifth person means Essentials. [All the prices side by side](/compare/).

## What Crisp does that Dinghy doesn't

- A free plan. Dinghy Cloud doesn't have one. Self-hosting is the free option.
- A shared email inbox, a knowledge base and more channels. Dinghy only emails your reply to a visitor who left, and they can answer from their mail app.
- A built-in AI chatbot on the higher plans.
- Native mobile apps. Dinghy's operator app is a web app you install on your phone, with push alerts.
- A marketplace of integrations. Dinghy has an API, webhooks and an MCP server instead.

## What Dinghy does differently

On Crisp Free, visitors can't send a screenshot or a file, and webhooks start at Essentials. Dinghy includes both, on the cloud and on your own server.

Crisp's plans count your contacts. Dinghy doesn't bill by contact. Dinghy Cloud is $9 a month for up to 5 people, no per-person add-ons.

Dinghy has no AI of its own, so no AI fee either. If you want a bot, connect your own agent through MCP or the API. Its replies wait as drafts until a person sends them, unless you let it send on its own. See [Bring your own AI agent](/blog/bring-your-own-ai-agent). But if you want a hosted bot that learns from your help center with zero setup, use Crisp.
