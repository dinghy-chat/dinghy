# Dinghy vs Drift

Drift is shutting down. Salesloft, which owns Drift, [announced a gradual sunset on March 5, 2026](https://www.salesloft.com/company/newsroom/1-mind-partnership) and named 1mind as the successor. Its [Drift sunset FAQ](https://help.salesloft.com/s/article/Drift-Sunset-FAQ) now gives the date. The Drift widget stops working after January 31, 2027, and your access ends then or when your contract ends, whichever comes first.

## Which one fits

If you used Drift as a sales tool, with buyer intent, lead routing to reps and Salesloft cadences, Dinghy won't replace that. Talk to Salesloft about 1mind, or look at another B2B sales chat tool.

If you used Drift as plain website chat, so visitors can reach a person on your team, you're paying for a sales platform to do a small job. That's when Dinghy fits. It's a chat widget and a shared inbox for one to five people, at $9 a month, or free on your own server.

## Price

| | Dinghy Cloud | Drift |
|---|---|---|
| Published price | $9 a month for up to 5 people | None. Salesloft's [pricing page](https://www.salesloft.com/pricing) only offers a sales call |
| Future of the product | Open source, you can host it yourself | Shuts down January 31, 2027, or sooner if your contract ends first |
| AI | None built in. Bring your own agent, no AI fee | AI chat agents, now through 1mind |
| Self-hosting | Free, open source | Not possible |

Neither Salesloft nor 1mind publishes a price for Drift or its successor. Check your contract end date too, because it may be your real deadline. [All the prices side by side](/compare/).

## What Drift does that Dinghy doesn't

- AI chat agents that qualify B2B leads and route high-intent visitors to sales reps, according to [Salesloft's page](https://www.salesloft.com/platform/drift).
- Buyer signals sent into Salesloft's sales workflow.
- Personalization from CRM data and past conversations.

## What Dinghy does differently

Dinghy is a support chat, not a sales platform. A visitor writes, your team gets a push or email alert, and whoever is free answers. You can send [proactive messages and campaigns](/blog/proactive-live-chat) to visitors on certain pages, which covers part of what Drift's playbooks did.

The price is on the page and doesn't depend on a contract. Dinghy Cloud is $9 a month for the workspace, and you can cancel anytime. Dinghy is open source, so if Dinghy Cloud ever stopped, you could keep running the same code on your own server.

If you want AI answers, connect your own agent through MCP or the API. Its replies start as drafts for a person to send, until you let it send on its own. See [Bring your own AI agent](/blog/bring-your-own-ai-agent).

## Moving over

Dinghy can't import Drift conversations. To get your data out, ask your Salesloft account manager or support for an export. If your contract runs past January 31, 2027, Salesloft keeps the data offline and takes export requests until April 30, 2027. It deletes everything on May 1, per the [sunset FAQ](https://help.salesloft.com/s/article/Drift-Sunset-FAQ). [Put Dinghy on your site](/compare/#moving-over-from-another-chat) before the Drift widget stops working.
