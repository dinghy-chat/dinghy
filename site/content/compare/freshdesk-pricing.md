# Freshdesk pricing in 2026

Freshdesk charges per agent, per month. There are two product lines. Freshdesk, for email support, is $19, $55 or $89 an agent a month billed annually. Freshdesk Omni, which adds chat and other channels, is $29, $79 or $119. Five agents on Omni Growth pay $145 a month before AI add-ons.

Prices are from [Freshdesk's pricing page](https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/pricing/) and [Freshdesk Omni's pricing page](https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/omni/pricing/), checked on November 27, 2026. Both pages list annual prices and say annual billing saves 20%, so monthly billing costs more.

## Freshdesk plans

| Plan | Per agent a month, billed annually | What it adds |
|---|---|---|
| Growth | $19 | Ticketing and a shared inbox, a customer portal and knowledge base, reports, roles and permissions |
| Pro | $55 | A multilingual help desk, custom dashboards, intelligent routing, multiple SLA policies, external collaborators |
| Enterprise | $89 | Freddy AI Insights, skill-based routing, a sandbox, audit logs, IP allowlists |

Freshworks describes these plans as service over email.

## Freshdesk Omni plans

| Plan | Per agent a month, billed annually | What it adds |
|---|---|---|
| Growth | $29 | Omnichannel support, a customer portal and knowledge base, reports and dashboards |
| Pro | $79 | A multilingual help desk, advanced analytics, intelligent routing, a team availability dashboard |
| Enterprise | $119 | Freddy AI Insights, skill-based routing, custom objects, a sandbox, audit logs |

If you want chat on your website and email in the same inbox, Omni is the line to price.

Every plan comes with a 14-day trial of Enterprise. Plain Freshdesk also has a [Free Program](https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/50000010099-explore-freshdesk-free-plan) for up to 2 agents, free for 6 months. Freshdesk Omni doesn't have one, and once you pay for a plan you can't go back to it.

## The charges people miss

**Freddy AI Agent sessions.** Each account gets 500 free AI Agent sessions, once. After that, sessions cost $49 per 100. A chat session covers everything within 24 hours of the start, and an email session covers 72 hours from the customer's first email. If the bot uses 600 sessions in a month after the free ones run out, that's six packs, or $294. If the customer talks to the bot again after those 24 hours, that's a new session.

**Freddy AI Copilot.** The AI assistant for agents is $29 per agent a month, on Pro and Enterprise only. That's more than a Freshdesk Growth seat.

**Day passes.** For an occasional helper, a day pass costs $2, $7 or $12 on Freshdesk Growth, Pro and Enterprise, and $5, $10 or $15 on Omni.

**Voice.** Phone on Omni is $15, $39 or $69 per agent a month on Growth, Pro and Enterprise, plus call rates.

**Connector tasks and MCP actions.** App connector tasks are $80 per 5,000, and MCP actions are $15 per 1,000.

## Cheaper options for email tickets or chat

If you only need email tickets and a knowledge base, Freshdesk Growth at $19 an agent is already one of the lower per-seat prices among help desks. [Help Scout](/compare/help-scout-pricing) has a free plan for up to 5 users and 25 contacts a month.

If you mostly need website chat, a help desk is more than you need. tawk.to is free, and Crisp and Chatwoot have free plans. See [all the live chat prices side by side](/compare/).

## When Freshdesk is worth it

Freshdesk is worth it when email tickets are your main channel and you want SLAs, routing and a knowledge base. Freshdesk Growth and Zendesk Support Team both start at $19 an agent on annual billing, but Growth includes the knowledge base. With chat added, Omni Growth at $29 an agent costs less than Zendesk Suite Team at $55. Omni makes sense when chat, email and phone need to land in one queue with one report. For a small team that only answers chat on its site, most of it goes unused.

## Dinghy, if you only need website chat

Dinghy is a small open-source live chat with a shared inbox. Dinghy Cloud is $9 a month for the workspace, for up to 5 people, and it's free on your own server. It has no tickets, SLAs, knowledge base, phone or built-in bot. If your support is mostly email, keep a help desk. You can connect your own AI agent, and its replies start as drafts for a person to check. See [Dinghy vs Freshdesk](/compare/freshdesk).

## FAQ

### How much is Freshdesk per agent?

Freshdesk is $19, $55 or $89 per agent a month on annual billing, for Growth, Pro and Enterprise. Freshdesk Omni is $29, $79 or $119.

### Does Freshdesk include live chat?

Freshworks sells chat and other channels with Freshdesk Omni. The plain Freshdesk plans are described as email support.

### How much does Freddy AI cost?

The AI Agent comes with 500 free sessions once per account, then $49 per 100 sessions. Freddy AI Copilot is $29 per agent a month on Pro and Enterprise.

### Is there a free trial?

Yes. You get 14 days of the Enterprise plan.
