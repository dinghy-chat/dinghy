# Dinghy vs Freshdesk

Freshdesk is a ticketing help desk built around email. For website chat, Freshworks sells two other products. Freshdesk Omni puts chat and tickets in one inbox, and Freshchat is chat on its own, with a free plan. Dinghy is only website chat and a shared inbox, at $9 a month for up to 5 people.

## Which one fits

Stay on Freshdesk if most of your support arrives by email, or you use its ticket rules, SLAs, customer portal or Freddy AI. If you want free chat for a few agents and don't mind the Freshworks ecosystem, Freshchat has a free plan for up to 10 agents. Pick Dinghy if website chat is the main thing you need, you're one to five people, and you'd rather pay $9 a month than a price per agent, or host it yourself.

## Price for a small team

| | Dinghy Cloud | Freshdesk Omni Growth |
|---|---|---|
| 1 person | $9 a month | $29 a month |
| 3 people | $9 a month | $87 a month |
| 5 people | $9 a month | $145 a month |
| Email tickets | No. Dinghy can email a reply the visitor hasn't read, if they gave their address | Included |
| AI | None built in. Bring your own agent, no AI fee | 500 Freddy AI Agent sessions once per account, then $49 per 100 sessions |
| Self-hosting | Free, open source | Not possible |
| Free trial | None | 14 days |

Freshdesk Omni prices are from [Freshdesk Omni's pricing page](https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/omni/pricing/), per agent billed annually. Plain [Freshdesk](https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/pricing/) starts at $19 an agent a month billed annually, and Freshworks describes that Growth plan as service over email. Its [Free Program](https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/50000010099-explore-freshdesk-free-plan) covers up to 2 agents free for 6 months. [Freshchat's pricing page](https://www.freshworks.com/live-chat-software/pricing/) lists a free plan for up to 10 agents with a chat widget, and paid plans from $19 an agent a month billed annually. More detail on the [Freshdesk pricing page](/compare/freshdesk-pricing). [All the prices side by side](/compare/).

## What Freshdesk does that Dinghy doesn't

- Email ticketing, a customer portal and a knowledge base.
- Freddy AI Agent, a hosted bot that answers by itself. Freddy AI Copilot for agents costs $29 an agent a month billed annually, on Pro and Enterprise.
- Phone, through a voice add-on on Omni.
- More channels in one inbox on Omni.
- The rest of the Freshworks suite, such as Freshsales for CRM.

## What Dinghy does differently

Dinghy Cloud is $9 a month for the workspace, up to 5 people. Adding your third or fifth person doesn't change the bill. There are no AI sessions to count, because Dinghy has no AI of its own. If you want a bot, connect your own agent through MCP or the API, and its replies start as drafts for a person to send, until you let it send on its own. See [Bring your own AI agent](/blog/bring-your-own-ai-agent).

Dinghy does one thing. If your team lives in email tickets, that's a gap, not a feature. If you mostly answer people on your website, it's less to set up. If $0 matters most, Freshchat Free beats Dinghy on price. Dinghy is for the team that wants one flat price past the free tier, or chat on its own server for free.
