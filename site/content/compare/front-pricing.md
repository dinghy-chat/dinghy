# Front pricing in 2026

Front charges per seat, per month. Starter is $25 a seat for up to 10 seats, Professional is $65 for up to 50, and Enterprise is $105. Those are annual billing prices. Front says annual billing saves 24%, so paying monthly costs more. Five people on Starter pay $125 a month.

Prices are from [Front's pricing page](https://front.com/pricing), checked on November 27, 2026.

## Every plan

| Plan | Per seat a month, billed annually | Seat limit | What it adds |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $25 | Up to 10 | A shared inbox and tickets for one channel type, up to 10 automation rules, basic analytics, a knowledge base |
| Professional | $65 | Up to 50 | Every channel type, macros and up to 20 rules, advanced analytics, up to 5 workspaces, SSO and SCIM |
| Enterprise | $105 | None listed | AI routing and tagging, unlimited rules, a multilingual knowledge base, custom roles, Copilot, QA and CSAT included |

The Starter limit is the one to read twice. Starter gives you a single channel type, which means email, chat or SMS. If you want website chat and email in the same inbox, you need Professional, and the price goes from $25 to $65 a seat.

The free trial is 14 days of Professional, no card needed.

## The charges people miss

**Copilot.** The AI assistant for your team is $20 per seat a month on Starter and Professional. It's included on Enterprise.

**Autopilot.** Front's AI that answers customers is an add-on on every plan. It costs [$0.39 when it hands a conversation to a person and $0.89 when it resolves it](https://help.front.com/en/articles/4890624), billed once per conversation at the higher rate it reached.

**WhatsApp.** On Professional and Enterprise, you pay Meta's WhatsApp fees plus a 20% admin fee.

**API limits.** A higher API rate limit is $200 a month per 100 extra requests a minute.

**Onboarding.** Front requires an onboarding package for contracts over $25,000. Its pricing FAQ links to [the packages and their prices](https://front.com/webassets/rebrand/Front_Success_Services_7_3.pdf).

## What 1, 3 and 5 people pay

| Seats | Starter | Starter with Copilot | Professional | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | $25 | $45 | $65 | $105 |
| 3 | $75 | $135 | $195 | $315 |
| 5 | $125 | $225 | $325 | $525 |

Per month on annual billing. Starter and Professional can start at one seat, but Front's [licensing FAQ](https://help.front.com/en/articles/2178) sends Enterprise buyers to sales for the seat minimum, so read that column as seat price times people. Autopilot conversations and WhatsApp fees come on top.

## Cheaper options for a shared inbox or chat

If you picked Front for a shared email inbox, [Help Scout](/compare/help-scout-pricing) starts at $25 a user on annual billing and includes live chat on Standard. [Freshdesk](/compare/freshdesk-pricing) Growth is $19 an agent for email tickets.

If you need website chat, you don't need Front. tawk.to is free, and Crisp and Chatwoot have free plans. See [all the live chat prices side by side](/compare/).

## When Front is worth it

Front is worth it when your team lives in email and works it together. Think sales, support and operations replying to customers from shared addresses, with comments, assignment and rules on top. Professional makes sense once chat, SMS and social need to share that inbox. If chat on your website is most of your support, you're paying for an email client you won't use much.

## Dinghy, if you only need website chat

Dinghy is a small open-source live chat with a shared inbox. Dinghy Cloud is $9 a month for the workspace, for up to 5 people, and it's free on your own server. It doesn't handle your email, SMS or social channels. If a visitor gave their email and left before reading your reply, Dinghy can email it to them, and their answer comes back into the chat. Some teams keep Front for email and use Dinghy for chat. See [Dinghy vs Front](/compare/front).

## FAQ

### How much does Front cost per user?

Starter is $25 a seat a month, Professional $65 and Enterprise $105, on annual billing. Monthly billing costs more.

### Does Front Starter include live chat?

Yes, if chat is your only channel type. Starter allows one channel type, either email, chat or SMS. For chat and email together, you need Professional.

### How much is Front's AI?

Copilot is $20 a seat a month on Starter and Professional, and included on Enterprise. Autopilot is $0.39 per handoff or $0.89 per resolution.

### Does Front have a free plan?

No. It has a 14-day free trial of Professional.
