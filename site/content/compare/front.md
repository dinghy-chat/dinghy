# Dinghy vs Front

Front is a shared inbox for email first, with chat, SMS and other channels on top. Dinghy is a website chat with a shared inbox for those chats. They overlap only in the chat part.

## Which one fits

Most Front users should stay on Front. If your team handles customer email together, with assignment, comments and rules, Dinghy doesn't do that.

Dinghy makes sense if you bought Front only for the chat widget on your website and barely use the email side. Then you're paying per seat for an email tool to run a chat. Dinghy costs $9 a month for up to five people, or nothing on your own server.

## What Front does that Dinghy doesn't

- A shared email inbox, with assignment, internal comments and rules. This is the main reason people use Front.
- Email, SMS and social in one inbox on Professional and up, with native WhatsApp as a paid add-on. Starter does SMS only if it's your one channel.
- Resolve, Front's chat widget, with self-serve flows and Autopilot AI answers.
- Many integrations with CRMs and other tools.

## What Dinghy does differently

Dinghy only does website chat, and it does it at one price for the team. The fifth person costs the same as the first. Visitors can send screenshots and files. If they gave their email and left before reading your answer, Dinghy can send it to their inbox, and they can reply from their mail app.

Dinghy has no AI of its own. If you want a bot, connect your own agent through MCP or the API. Its replies start as drafts for a person to send, until you let it send on its own. See [Bring your own AI agent](/blog/bring-your-own-ai-agent).
