# Gorgias pricing in 2026

Gorgias charges by the number of tickets you handle each month, not per agent. Starter is $10 a month for 50 tickets. Basic is $60 a month, or $50 a month on annual billing, for 300 tickets. Pro is $360 or $300 for 2,000 tickets. Each ticket past your allowance costs $0.36 to $0.40.

Prices are from [Gorgias's pricing page](https://www.gorgias.com/pricing), checked on November 27, 2026.

## Every plan

| Plan | Billed monthly | Billed annually, per month | Tickets a month | Each extra ticket | AI interactions with the paid add-on |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $10 | Monthly only | 50 | $0.40 | 30 |
| Basic | $60 | $50 | 300 | $0.40 | 30 |
| Pro | $360 | $300 | 2,000 | $0.36 | 190 |
| Advanced | $900 | $750 | 5,000 | $0.36 | 530 |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Over 5,000 | Custom | Custom |

Gorgias describes Starter as for brands getting started on one platform, Basic for brands whose volume is outgrowing their team, and Pro for brands that let AI resolve and sell, with revenue analytics. Advanced goes through a demo. Annual plans are paid upfront for 12 months.

The free trial is 7 days, no payment details needed. During the trial, outgoing email is capped at 20 messages a day and 600 in total, and you can't create a help center, per [Gorgias's trial article](https://docs.gorgias.com/en-US/create-a-gorgias-helpdesk-82010).

## How tickets are counted

A ticket becomes billable once Gorgias sends a message in it, from a person on your team, a rule or the AI Agent. A shopper's reply to a chat campaign also opens a billable ticket. Voice and SMS are billed separately. Replies in the same thread count once. A customer who writes back more than 10 days after the last email, or more than 3 days after the last chat message, opens a new ticket. It counts toward your allowance once Gorgias sends a reply in it, like any other ticket. See [Gorgias's billing rules](https://docs.gorgias.com/en-US/how-youre-billed-for-using-gorgias-199385). Spam, newsletters and auto-responders are filtered out and not billed.

This matters because a rule that auto-replies to every message turns each one into a billable ticket.

## The charges people miss

**AI Agent.** It's an add-on on every plan. Gorgias quotes $0.90 per resolved conversation on most plans and from $1 on Starter, per [its AI pricing breakdown](https://www.gorgias.com/blog/ai-agent-pricing). Past the included interactions, the Support + Shopping Assistant package costs $1.50 each. The support-only package costs $1.00 to $2.00 monthly or $0.83 to $1.67 on annual plans, depending on the tier. When AI Agent settles a ticket with no person stepping in, you pay the automation fee and the ticket counts toward your helpdesk allowance. A Flow or other automation that settles a question without a ticket costs only the automation fee.

**Overage in a busy month.** On Basic, a month with 500 tickets costs $60 plus 200 extra tickets at $0.40, so $140. A sale or a shipping delay can do that.

**Voice and SMS.** Gorgias adds them to any plan, priced by volume. The pricing page doesn't print the rates.

## What 1, 3 and 5 people pay

Gorgias doesn't price per agent, so team size doesn't change the bill. Ticket volume does. The page says the number of user seats depends on the plan.

| Tickets a month | Plan | Per month, billed monthly | Per month, billed annually |
|---|---|---|---|
| 50 | Starter | $10 | Monthly only |
| 100 | Starter plus 50 extra tickets | $30 | Monthly only |
| 300 | Basic | $60 | $50 |
| 1,000 | Basic plus 700 extra tickets | $340 | $330 |
| 2,000 | Pro | $360 | $300 |

These totals cover the helpdesk. If you turn on AI Agent, add its package and any overage.

At around 1,000 tickets a month, Basic with overage costs about what Pro costs. Check that line before you pick a plan.

## Cheaper options for store chat

If you're a store with low volume, Starter at $10 is cheap as long as you stay near 50 tickets. If your volume swings, a flat price is easier to plan for. tawk.to is free. [Tidio](/compare/tidio-pricing) has a free plan and Shopify features, and it also counts conversations. See [all the live chat prices side by side](/compare/).

## When Gorgias is worth it

Gorgias is worth it for an e-commerce store on Shopify that wants order details next to every conversation, refunds and edits from the inbox, and revenue reports on support. Billing by ticket is fair when one or two people answer a lot of chats, since you don't pay per seat. It gets expensive in busy months, and the AI Agent adds a charge per interaction on top of the ticket.

## Dinghy, if you only need website chat

Dinghy is a small open-source live chat with a shared inbox. Dinghy Cloud is $9 a month for the workspace, for up to 5 people, with no ticket or conversation count. It's free on your own server. It has no Shopify order actions, no email inbox, no social channels and no built-in bot, so it doesn't replace Gorgias for a store that relies on those. ## FAQ

### How much does Gorgias cost per month?

Starter is $10 for 50 tickets. Basic is $60 a month, or $50 on annual billing, for 300 tickets. Pro is $360 or $300 for 2,000, and Advanced $900 or $750 for 5,000.

### Does Gorgias charge per user?

No. Gorgias bills by tickets. The number of user seats depends on the plan.

### What happens if I go over my ticket limit?

Each extra ticket costs $0.40 on Starter and Basic, and $0.36 on Pro and Advanced.

### How much is the Gorgias AI Agent?

It's a paid add-on. Gorgias quotes $0.90 per resolved conversation on most plans and from $1 on Starter. [Overage rates](https://www.gorgias.com/blog/ai-agent-pricing) depend on the package. A conversation AI Agent resolves on its own also counts as a ticket.
