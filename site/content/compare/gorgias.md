# Dinghy vs Gorgias

Gorgias is a help desk for online stores. It pulls orders from Shopify and other platforms into each conversation, and it handles email, chat, social and a help center in one place. Dinghy is a website chat with a shared inbox. It knows nothing about your orders.

## Which one fits

If you run a store and your team handles orders, refunds and returns over email and chat, stay on Gorgias. Seeing the order next to the message and acting on it from there is the point of Gorgias, and Dinghy can't do it.

Dinghy fits if you only use the chat part of Gorgias, or your store gets more chats than Gorgias's ticket allowance covers and you don't need the order tools. Dinghy has no ticket count. Every chat is included in $9 a month.

## Price

Gorgias charges by ticket, not by person. Every chat or email that a person, a rule or the AI Agent answers counts as one ticket, and so does a shopper's reply to a chat campaign.

| | Dinghy Cloud | Gorgias |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 50 conversations a month | $9 a month | $10 a month on Starter, 3 seats |
| 300 conversations a month | $9 a month | $60 a month on Basic |
| 1,000 conversations a month | $9 a month | $340 a month on Basic with overage, or $360 on Pro |
| Team size | Up to 5 people | 3 seats on Starter, 500 from Basic |
| AI | None built in. Bring your own agent, no AI fee | Paid AI Agent add-on, priced by package. A conversation it resolves alone also counts as a ticket |
| Self-hosting | Free, open source | Not possible |

Gorgias prices are from [Gorgias's pricing page](https://www.gorgias.com/pricing), billed monthly. Starter includes 50 tickets and Basic 300, and both charge $0.40 for each extra one. Pro includes 2,000. Chats, emails and social messages all count toward the same allowance. More detail on the [Gorgias pricing page](/compare/gorgias-pricing). [All the prices side by side](/compare/).

For a small store with up to three people and under 50 tickets a month, counting email and chat together, Gorgias Starter costs $1 more than Dinghy Cloud and gives you the order tools.

## What Gorgias does that Dinghy doesn't

- Order details from Shopify, WooCommerce and [BigCommerce](https://docs.gorgias.com/en-US/connect-bigcommerce-to-gorgias-296165) on every plan, and Magento from Pro.
- Email, social, a help center and chat in one inbox.
- An AI Agent built for ecommerce, as a paid add-on on every plan.
- Revenue attribution on Pro and up.
- Voice and SMS add-ons.

## What Dinghy does differently

A busy month doesn't change the Dinghy bill. Dinghy Cloud is $9 a month for up to 5 people, however many chats come in. The [widget loader](/live-chat-widget) is under 4 KB gzipped. The full chat loads when a visitor opens it, comes back to a chat, gets identified by your store, or has a message waiting. Add it to a Shopify store with the [Shopify guide](/guides/shopify).

Dinghy has no AI of its own. If you want a bot, connect your own agent through MCP or the API. Its replies start as drafts for a person to send, until you let it send on its own. See [Bring your own AI agent](/blog/bring-your-own-ai-agent) and [Dinghy for online stores](/for/ecommerce).

## Moving over

Dinghy can't import Gorgias tickets. [Switch your site to Dinghy](/compare/#moving-over-from-another-chat).
