# Help Scout pricing in 2026

Help Scout has a free plan for up to 5 users and 25 contacts a month, without live chat. Live chat starts on Standard, at $25 a user a month on annual billing or $30 on monthly billing. Plus is $45 or $54, and Pro is $75 or $90. Five people on Standard pay $125 to $150 a month.

Prices are from [Help Scout's pricing page](https://www.helpscout.com/pricing/), checked on November 27, 2026.

## Every plan

| Plan | Billed annually | Billed monthly | Users | What it adds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 | Up to 5 | 1 inbox, 1 docs site, 25 contacts a month, 10 saved replies |
| Standard | $25 per user a month | $30 per user a month | Up to 25 | Unlimited contacts, 2 inboxes, live chat and SMS, WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger, one SLA policy |
| Plus | $45 per user a month | $54 per user a month | Up to 50 | 5 inboxes, advanced workflows, two SLA policies, a customer portal, Salesforce, Jira and HubSpot |
| Pro | $75 per user a month | $90 per user a month | 10 minimum | 10 inboxes, unlimited workflows and SLAs, SSO, HIPAA, a dedicated onboarding specialist |

A contact is someone who got a reply from your team or an answer from the AI. Paid plans have no contact limit.

Standard and Plus have a 15-day free trial, no card needed. Pro goes through sales.

## The charges people miss

**AI resolutions.** Help Scout's AI agent is an add-on called Envoy, at $0.75 per resolution, billed monthly. A resolution is a conversation the AI closes without a person. You don't pay when the customer asks for more help. New accounts get 3 months of unlimited resolutions free, and you can set a monthly cap. On the Free plan, each AI answer still counts toward the 25 contacts. When you hit the cap, the AI agent stops until the next cycle.

The free months make the first bill look small. If the AI resolves 300 conversations a month, month four costs $225 more than month three.

**Extra inboxes.** Each inbox past your plan's allowance is $10 a month on annual billing or $12 on monthly billing.

**Extra docs sites.** Each extra knowledge base site is $20 a month on annual billing or $24 on monthly billing.

**Pro's minimum.** Pro needs at least 10 users. At $75 a user, that's $750 a month even for a team of 5.

Per month. The lower number is annual billing, the higher one monthly billing. AI resolutions, extra inboxes and docs sites come on top.

## Free and cheaper options

The Free plan is a real option if you answer fewer than 25 people a month by email. Once you hit 25, you can't reply to customers until the next cycle or until you upgrade, per [Help Scout's free plan article](https://docs.helpscout.com/article/1597-free-plan). Standard is the first paid step.

If chat on your site is most of what you need, tawk.to is free, and Crisp and Chatwoot have free plans. See [all the live chat prices side by side](/compare/). [Freshdesk](/compare/freshdesk-pricing) Growth is $19 an agent a month on annual billing for email tickets.

## When Help Scout is worth it

Help Scout is worth it when most of your support comes by email and you want it to feel like email to your customers, with a knowledge base next to it. Standard puts chat, SMS and social messages in the same place at $25 a user.

## Dinghy, if you only need website chat

Dinghy is a small open-source live chat with a shared inbox. Dinghy Cloud is $9 a month for the workspace, for up to 5 people, and it's free on your own server. It has no email inbox or knowledge base, so it won't replace Help Scout for a team that works by email. If a visitor gave their email and left before reading your reply, Dinghy can email it to them. Some teams keep Help Scout for email and use Dinghy for chat. See [Dinghy vs Help Scout](/compare/help-scout).

## FAQ

### How much does Help Scout cost?

Standard is $25 a user a month on annual billing or $30 on monthly billing. Plus is $45 or $54, and Pro is $75 or $90 with a 10 user minimum. There is also a free plan.

### What's in the Help Scout free plan?

Up to 5 users, 1 inbox, 1 docs site and 25 contacts a month.

### How much is Help Scout's AI?

The Envoy add-on is $0.75 per resolution. New accounts get 3 months of unlimited resolutions free, and you can set a monthly cap. On the Free plan, AI answers count toward the 25 contacts.

### Does Help Scout include live chat?

Yes, from Standard up. The Free plan doesn't include it. Standard lists live chat and SMS, plus WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger.
