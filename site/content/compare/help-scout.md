# Dinghy vs Help Scout

Help Scout is an email help desk first. Shared inboxes, a knowledge base, and a widget called Beacon that can do live chat. Dinghy only does website chat.

## The quick answer

Use Help Scout if most of your support comes by email and you also need chat and docs. Dinghy is $7 a month for five people or fewer. Use it if you need chat on your site.

## Price for a small team

| | Dinghy Cloud | Help Scout Standard |
|---|---|---|
| 1 person | $7 a month | $25 a month |
| 3 people | $7 a month | $75 a month |
| 5 people | $7 a month | $125 a month |
| Live chat on the free plan | No free plan | No. Free has 5 users and email, but no live chat |
| Email inbox and knowledge base | No. Replies go by email to a visitor who left | Yes |
| AI answers | None built in. Your own agent drafts replies, no AI fee | $0.75 per resolution, if you turn them on |
| Self-hosting | Free, open source | Not possible |
| Free trial | None | 15 days, no card |

Help Scout prices are from helpscout.com/pricing on November 9, 2026. The $25 is per user, billed yearly, which is what the page shows by default. Monthly costs more. AI answers are $0.75 per resolution if you turn them on. [All the prices side by side](/compare/).

## What Help Scout does that Dinghy doesn't

- A shared email inbox.
- A knowledge base, and the Beacon widget that puts help articles and contact options on your site.
- SMS, WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger on Standard.
- Built-in AI answers and workflows.
- A free plan, for email only.

If your customers mostly email you, use Help Scout.

## What Dinghy does differently

A visitor clicks the chat button and your team answers from a shared inbox, with push alerts on their phones. If the visitor left and gave an email, your reply goes to their inbox, and their answer comes back into the same conversation. You get saved replies, notes, labels, files and business hours. You can connect your own AI agent too, and its replies wait as drafts for your team to send. The widget loader is under 4 KB, and the rest only loads when someone opens it.

Dinghy Cloud charges per workspace. Dinghy Cloud is $7 a month for up to 5 people. You pay at signup and can cancel anytime.

Some teams keep Help Scout for email and use Dinghy for chat.

## Moving over

There's no importer from Help Scout. If you only want to move chat, add the Dinghy tag and turn chat off in Beacon, or take Beacon off the site. The steps are in [Moving over from another chat](/compare/#moving-over-from-another-chat).
