# Dinghy vs Help Scout

Help Scout is an email help desk first. It has shared inboxes, a knowledge base, and a widget called Beacon that can do live chat. Dinghy only does website chat. So the real question is whether you need a help desk or just a chat.

## The quick answer

Pick Help Scout if most of your support arrives by email and you want chat, docs and email in one place. Pick Dinghy if chat on your site is the main thing, you're a team of up to five, and you'd rather pay $7 a month than per user.

## Price for a small team

| | Dinghy Cloud | Help Scout Standard |
|---|---|---|
| 1 person | $7 a month | $25 a month |
| 3 people | $7 a month | $75 a month |
| 5 people | $7 a month | $125 a month |
| Live chat on the free plan | No free plan | No. Free has 5 users and email, but no live chat |
| Email inbox and knowledge base | No. Replies go by email to a visitor who left | Yes |
| AI answers | None built in. Your own agent drafts replies, no AI fee | $0.75 per resolution, if you turn them on |
| Self-hosting | Free, open source | Not possible |
| Free trial | None | 15 days, no card |

Help Scout prices are from helpscout.com/pricing on November 9, 2026. The $25 is per user billed yearly, which the page shows by default. Paying monthly costs more. Help Scout's AI answers cost $0.75 per resolution if you turn them on. [All the prices side by side](/compare/).

## What Help Scout does that Dinghy doesn't

- A shared email inbox, which is its core.
- A knowledge base, and the Beacon widget that puts help articles and contact options on your site.
- SMS, WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger on Standard.
- Built-in AI answers and workflows.
- A free plan, for email only.

If your customers mostly email you, Help Scout is the better tool, and chat is a nice extra.

## What Dinghy does differently

Dinghy does one thing. A visitor clicks the chat button, and your team answers from a shared inbox, with push alerts on their phones. If the visitor left and gave an email, your reply goes to their inbox, and their answer comes back into the same conversation. Saved replies, notes, labels, files and business hours are all there. You can also connect your own AI agent, whose replies wait as drafts for your team to send. The widget's loader is under 4 KB, and the rest loads only when someone opens it.

The price doesn't depend on users. Dinghy Cloud is $7 a month for up to 5 people. You pay at signup and can cancel anytime.

Some teams keep Help Scout for email and use Dinghy for chat. That works fine, since the two don't need to know about each other.

## Moving over

There's no importer from Help Scout. If you only want to move chat, add the Dinghy tag and take chat out of your Beacon, or remove Beacon from the site. [Moving over from another chat](/compare/#moving-over-from-another-chat) has the steps.
