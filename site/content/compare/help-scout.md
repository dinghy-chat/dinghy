# Dinghy vs Help Scout

Help Scout is an email help desk first. Shared inboxes, a knowledge base, and a widget called Beacon that can do live chat. Dinghy only does website chat.

## Which one fits

Use Help Scout if most of your support comes by email and you also need chat and docs. Dinghy is $9 a month for five people or fewer. Use it if you need chat on your site.

## Price for a small team

| | Dinghy Cloud | Help Scout Standard |
|---|---|---|
| 1 person | $9 a month | $25 to $30 a month |
| 3 people | $9 a month | $75 to $90 a month |
| 5 people | $9 a month | $125 to $150 a month |
| Live chat on the free plan | No free plan | No. Free has email and a knowledge base, with 25 contacts per month |
| Email inbox and knowledge base | No. Replies go by email to a visitor who left | Yes |
| AI answers | None built in. Your own agent drafts replies, no AI fee | $0.75 per resolution, if you turn them on |
| Self-hosting | Free, open source | Not possible |
| Free trial | None | 15 days, no card |

Help Scout prices are from [Help Scout’s pricing page](https://www.helpscout.com/pricing/). Standard costs $25 per user per month on annual billing and $30 on monthly billing. AI answers are $0.75 per resolution if you turn them on. [All the prices side by side](/compare/).

## What Help Scout does that Dinghy doesn't

- A shared email inbox.
- A knowledge base, and the Beacon widget that puts help articles and contact options on your site.
- SMS, WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger on Standard.
- Built-in AI answers and workflows.
- A free plan for email and help articles.

If your customers mostly email you, use Help Scout.

## What Dinghy does differently

A visitor clicks the chat button and your team answers from a shared inbox, with push alerts on their phones. If the visitor left and gave an email, your reply goes to their inbox, and their answer comes back into the same conversation. You get saved replies, notes, labels, files and business hours. You can connect your own AI agent too, and its replies wait as drafts for your team to send. The [widget loader](/live-chat-widget) is under 4 KB.

Dinghy Cloud costs $9 a month per workspace for up to five people.

Some teams keep Help Scout for email and use Dinghy for chat.

## Moving over

Dinghy can’t import Help Scout conversations. You can turn off chat in Beacon and keep the rest of Beacon on your site. [Switch your site to Dinghy](/compare/#moving-over-from-another-chat).
