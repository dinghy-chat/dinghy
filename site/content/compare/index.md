# What live chat costs for a small team

I checked each company's pricing page for the cost of 1, 3 and 5 people.

## The table

Monthly prices in US dollars for the plans below.

| Product | 1 person | 3 people | 5 people | The catch |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dinghy Cloud | $7 | $7 | $7 | Up to 5 people, 1 GB of files kept 12 months, no built-in AI |
| Dinghy, self-hosted | $0 | $0 | $0 | Plus your server, and you run it |
| tawk.to | $0 | $0 | $0 | Shows "Powered by tawk.to" unless you pay $29 a month |
| Crisp Free, then Mini | $0 | $45 | $95 | Free is 2 people and 100 contacts. Mini stops at 4, so 5 people need Essentials |
| Tidio Starter | $24 | $24 | $24 | 100 conversations a month, and the Tidio branding stays |
| Chatwoot Startups | $19 | $57 | $95 | $19 per agent. The free Hacker plan has 2 agents and 500 conversations |
| Intercom Essential | $19 to $29 | $57 to $87 | $95 to $145 | $19 a seat for new customers, $29 list price, plus $0.99 per Fin AI outcome if you turn Fin on |
| LiveChat Essential | $19 to $25 | $57 to $75 | $95 to $125 | Per seat, yearly or monthly. 60 days of chat history |
| Help Scout Standard | $25 | $75 | $125 | Per user, billed yearly. No live chat on the free plan |
| Olark Live Chat | $29 | $87 | $145 | Per agent. The AI plan starts at $400 a month |
| Zendesk Suite Team | $55 to $69 | $165 to $207 | $275 to $345 | Per agent, yearly or monthly. Live chat starts on Suite plans |

I read each pricing page on November 8, 2026, and the LiveChat, Help Scout, Olark and Zendesk ones again on November 9, then multiplied the per-seat prices out. For LiveChat and Zendesk the lower number is the yearly price. Intercom's page doesn't say if its price is yearly. Tidio's page has a yearly option, so the monthly rate may be higher. Tidio's $24 is $24.17 rounded. Check the vendor's page before you pick, and [tell me](mailto:hello@dinghy.chat) if I got one wrong.

## What the table doesn't show

If you're alone and don't mind a "powered by" badge, tawk.to is free. Crisp's free plan covers two people.

Per-seat plans charge for each person you add. Hit the conversation cap and you'll need a bigger plan. Dinghy Cloud's price doesn't move with either.

## Moving over from another chat

Dinghy can't import from other chat tools. Your old conversations stay where they are.

1. Set up Dinghy and copy your tag from Settings > Install.
2. Take the old chat's snippet off your site and paste the Dinghy tag where it was, usually just before `</body>`. The [install guides](/guides/) show where that is on each platform.
3. Add your site under **Allowed sites** in Settings > Install.
4. Keep the old account for a few weeks so you can answer what's still open there. Then cancel it.

If your old chat showed who was signed in, Dinghy does that too, with a short token your server signs. See [Show who is chatting](/docs/add-to-your-site#show-who-is-chatting).

To leave Dinghy, an owner can download a workspace export from Settings > Workspace.
