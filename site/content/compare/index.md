# What live chat costs for a small team

Here’s what the plans below cost each month for one, three or five people. Prices are in US dollars.

| Product | 1 person | 3 people | 5 people | The catch |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dinghy Cloud | $9 | $9 | $9 | Up to 5 people, 1 GB of files kept 12 months, no built-in AI |
| Dinghy, self-hosted | $0 | $0 | $0 | Plus your server, and you run it |
| tawk.to | $0 | $0 | $0 | Shows "Powered by tawk.to" unless you pay $29 a month |
| Crisp Free, then Mini | $0 | $45 | $95 | Free is 2 people and 100 contacts. Mini stops at 4, so 5 people need Essentials |
| Tidio Starter, Human only | $24 to $29 | $24 to $29 | $24 to $29 | 100 conversations a month at the listed price |
| Chatwoot Hacker, then Startups | $0 | $57 | $95 | Hacker is chat only, with 2 agents, 500 conversations a month and 30 days of history |
| Intercom Essential | $19 to $39 | $57 to $117 | $95 to $195 | $19 a seat on a limited new-customer offer, annual billing, $29 list price, $39 month to month. Plus Fin charges from $0.99 per outcome if you turn it on |
| Text Essential (LiveChat) | $19 to $25 | $57 to $75 | $95 to $125 | Per seat, yearly or monthly. 60 days of chat history |
| Help Scout Standard | $25 to $30 | $75 to $90 | $125 to $150 | Per user, yearly or monthly. No live chat on the free plan |
| Olark Live Chat | $29 | $87 | $145 | Per agent. The AI plan starts at $400 a month |
| Zendesk Suite Team | $55 to $69 | $165 to $207 | $275 to $345 | Per agent, yearly or monthly. Live chat starts on Suite plans |

I checked vendor prices on November 22, 2026, and Intercom again on November 27. For Tidio, LiveChat, Help Scout and Zendesk, the lower number is the monthly equivalent of annual billing. Intercom's range runs from the limited-time new-customer annual price to the month-to-month price. I used Chatwoot's first paid plan for teams too large for Hacker, and Crisp's Essentials plan for five people. Each comparison links to its source. Prices exclude taxes and optional add-ons.

## What the table doesn't show

If you're alone and don't mind a "powered by" badge, tawk.to is free. Crisp's free plan covers two people.

Per-seat plans charge for each person you add. Hit the conversation cap and you'll need a bigger plan. Dinghy Cloud stays $9 a month for up to 5 people, however many chats come in.

## Moving over from another chat

Dinghy can't import from other chat tools. Your old conversations stay where they are.

1. Set up Dinghy and copy your tag from Settings > Install.
2. Add your site under **Allowed sites** in Settings > Install, so the chat works the moment the tag goes live.
3. Take the old chat's snippet off your site and paste the Dinghy tag where it was, usually just before `</body>`. The [install guides](/guides/) show where that is on each platform. Then send yourself a test message.
4. Keep the old account for a few weeks so you can answer what's still open there. Then cancel it.

If your old chat showed who was signed in, Dinghy does that too, with a short token your server signs. See [Show who is chatting](/docs/add-to-your-site#show-who-is-chatting).

To leave Dinghy, an owner can download a workspace export from Settings > Workspace.
