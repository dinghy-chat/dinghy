# Intercom pricing in 2026

Intercom charges per seat, and then per Fin outcome if you use its AI agent. Essential lists at $29 a seat a month, and eligible new customers can get it for $19 during a [limited offer](https://www.intercom.com/legal/terms-and-policies/promotional-offer). Advanced is $85 and Expert is $132. On every plan, Fin charges $0.99 for a support answer over chat or email. Sales qualifications and voice cost more. Five people on Essential pay $95 to $145 a month on annual billing before Fin, or $195 month to month.

The prices below are from [Intercom's pricing page](https://www.intercom.com/pricing), checked on November 27, 2026. They are the annual billing prices. Month to month, Essential is $39, Advanced $99 and Expert $139 a seat, per [Intercom's plans article](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/9061614-fin-and-intercom-plans-explained).

## Every plan

| Plan | Per seat a month, billed annually | What it adds |
|---|---|---|
| Essential | $29, or $19 during the new-customer offer | Messenger live chat, a shared inbox and tickets, a public help center, prebuilt reports, basic automation |
| Advanced | $85 | A workflow builder, several team inboxes, round robin assignment, a private and multilingual help center, 20 free Lite seats |
| Expert | $132 | SSO, HIPAA support, SLAs, several brands on one Messenger and help center, 50 free Lite seats |

Lite seats are free on Advanced and Expert. They're for teammates who need to see and comment, not answer full time.

The free trial is 14 days, no card needed.

## The charges people miss

**Fin, per outcome.** Fin charges $0.99 for most outcomes on chat and email. An outcome is a customer saying the issue is resolved, a customer not asking for more help after Fin answers, or Fin finishing a workflow, handoffs included. Intercom charges at most once per conversation, and nothing when a customer asks for a person. If you use Fin with another help desk, [Fin's pricing page](https://fin.ai/pricing) sets a $49 a month minimum that includes 50 outcomes. Fin Voice is $1.99 per voice outcome, and a sales qualification, such as Fin booking a call, is $9.99, per [Intercom's article on Fin outcomes](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/8205718-fin-ai-agent-outcomes).

Fin's bill follows your volume, not your team. If Fin settles 400 chats in a month, that's $396 on top of the seats.

**Copilot.** The AI assistant for your team is $29 per teammate a month billed annually, or $35 billed monthly. Each agent gets 10 Copilot conversations a month free.

**Proactive Support Plus.** Targeted chats, banners and tooltips are included on every plan. Posts, series and product tours need this add-on, from $99 a month for the workspace with 500 messages sent. More messages cost extra.

**Pro.** Conversation analysis is from $100 a month for the workspace, with 500 conversations analyzed.

**Phone, SMS, WhatsApp and bulk email.** These are billed by usage. The pricing page doesn't print the rates.

## What 1, 3 and 5 people pay

| Seats | Essential | Advanced | Expert |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | $19 to $29 | $85 | $132 |
| 3 | $57 to $87 | $255 | $396 |
| 5 | $95 to $145 | $425 | $660 |

Per month on annual billing. The $19 Essential price lasts for the offer period shown at checkout. After that, Intercom charges its list price. Fin outcomes, Copilot and other add-ons come on top.

Startups can apply for [Intercom's Early Stage program](https://www.intercom.com/early-stage), for companies with up to $10M in funding, fewer than 15 employees and no current Intercom account. The discount drops each year for three years.

## Cheaper ways to get chat or an AI agent

If you want Intercom for the Messenger, you're paying for a lot of software around it. tawk.to is free, Crisp and Chatwoot have free plans, and Tidio starts at $24 a month for up to 10 seats. See [all the live chat prices side by side](/compare/).

If you want an AI agent, Fin's per-outcome price is the same on every plan, so you can keep the seat count low. You can also run your own agent against a cheaper chat through its API.

## When Intercom is worth it

Intercom earns its price when you want a hosted AI agent that learns from your help center and answers on its own, and you have the volume for $0.99 an outcome to beat a person's time. It also fits product teams that use tours, banners and targeted messages to onboard users. For a few people answering website chat by hand, Essential is most of what you need, and that's still per seat.

## Dinghy, if you only need website chat

Dinghy is a small open-source live chat with a shared inbox. Dinghy Cloud is $9 a month for the workspace, for up to 5 people, with no per-seat or per-outcome billing. You can also run it free on your own server. It has no hosted bot, help center, product tours or phone, so it's no replacement for a team that uses those. You can connect your own AI agent through the API or MCP, and you pay your model provider directly. See [Dinghy vs Intercom](/compare/intercom).

## FAQ

### How much does Intercom cost per month?

Essential is $29 a seat a month on annual billing, or $19 during the new-customer offer. Advanced is $85 and Expert is $132. Month to month, they're $39, $99 and $139. Fin charges start at $0.99 per outcome.

### How much does Intercom Fin cost?

$0.99 per support outcome on chat and email, on every plan. Voice outcomes are $1.99 and sales qualifications $9.99. You pay at most once per conversation and nothing when the customer asks for a person. Used with another help desk, Fin has a $49 monthly minimum with 50 outcomes included.

### Does Intercom have a free plan?

No. It has a 14-day free trial, no card needed.

### Can I use Intercom without Fin?

Yes. Fin is billed only when it produces an outcome, so if you don't turn it on, you pay for seats and any add-ons.
