# Intercom vs Zendesk for a small team

For a small team that wants website chat and email, Intercom costs less. Intercom Essential includes chat, email and a help center for $29 a seat a month billed annually or $39 month to month, and eligible new customers can get $19 on annual billing for a limited time. Zendesk's chat starts on Suite Team at $55 an agent a month billed yearly. Intercom is built around chat and its AI agent, Fin. Zendesk is built around tickets.

## Price for 1, 3 and 5 agents

On the cheapest plan with website chat and email:

| | Intercom Essential | Zendesk Suite Team |
|---|---|---|
| 1 agent | $19 to $39 a month | $55 to $69 a month |
| 3 agents | $57 to $117 a month | $165 to $207 a month |
| 5 agents | $95 to $195 a month | $275 to $345 a month |
| Free trial | 14 days, no card | 14 days |

Intercom's lower number is the limited-time new-customer price on annual billing, from [Intercom's pricing page](https://www.intercom.com/pricing). The higher one is month to month, from [Intercom's plans article](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/9061614-fin-and-intercom-plans-explained). The standard annual price sits between them at $29. Zendesk's lower number is billed yearly and the higher one monthly, from [Zendesk's pricing page](https://www.zendesk.com/pricing/). More detail on the [Intercom pricing page](/compare/intercom-pricing) and the [Zendesk pricing page](/compare/zendesk-pricing).

## Chat

Intercom Essential includes the Messenger for live chat on your site. Zendesk lists messaging and live chat from Suite Team. Its cheaper Support Team plan, $19 an agent billed yearly, doesn't list chat.

Intercom includes targeted chats, banners and tooltips on every plan. Product tours, surveys and message series need [Proactive Support Plus](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/9061648-proactive-support-plus-add-on), from $99 a month for the workspace with 500 messages sent.

## Email

Both include a shared inbox for email. Zendesk's ticketing goes further, with views, triggers and SLAs. Intercom handles email in the same inbox as chat, which suits a team whose email volume is modest.

## AI and what it costs

| | Intercom | Zendesk |
|---|---|---|
| Bot answers | Fin, $0.99 per support resolution on chat or email, on every plan. Sales qualifications and voice cost more | $2 of resolution credit per agent a month on Suite Team for accounts [bought or renewed after May 18, 2026](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/11504709759898-How-can-I-tell-which-pricing-model-I-have), then a price per resolution tier. Older accounts get 5 resolutions per agent, then $1.50 to $2.00 each |
| Help for agents | Copilot, 10 free conversations per agent a month, then $29 an agent a month billed annually or $35 monthly | Copilot, $50 an agent a month billed yearly |

Intercom bills Fin per outcome. Zendesk includes some credit per agent each month, then bills each resolution. Either way, a busy month with the bot on means a bigger bill. Both let you cap it, so set a limit before you turn the bot on.

## Setup

Intercom is quicker to get running for chat. Install the Messenger and connect your email. Fin answers from what you give it, such as help center articles, pages on your website or PDFs, so plan time to point it at good sources and check its answers. Zendesk takes more setup. Expect groups, views, triggers and macros before the widget goes on your site. It pays off if you'll grow into those tools. Both offer a 14-day trial.

## Who each one suits

Pick Intercom if chat is your main channel, you sell software, and you want Fin to answer common questions. Watch the seat count and the Fin outcomes as you grow.

Pick Zendesk if email tickets are most of your work, you want phone in the same place, or you expect a larger support team with routing and SLAs.

## If you only need website chat

Both bill per seat and both push AI. If your team is one to five people answering chats on your own website, Dinghy is a smaller option at $9 a month for the workspace, or free on your own server. It has no AI of its own, so you bring your own agent if you want one. See [Dinghy vs Intercom](/compare/intercom) and [Dinghy vs Zendesk](/compare/zendesk).
