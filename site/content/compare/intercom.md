# Dinghy vs Intercom

Intercom is a huge customer service platform with an AI agent at its center. Dinghy is a small live chat with a shared inbox. If you're a team of one to five and you mostly want visitors to reach a human, you probably don't need Intercom.

## The quick answer

Pick Dinghy if you want a chat on your site, a shared inbox, alerts, and a price that doesn't move when you hire. Pick Intercom if you want an AI agent answering customers, product tours, a help center, and email and phone support in one place, and you have the budget for it.

## Price for a small team

| | Dinghy Cloud | Intercom Essential |
|---|---|---|
| 1 person | $7 a month | $19 to $29 a month |
| 3 people | $7 a month | $57 to $87 a month |
| 5 people | $7 a month | $95 to $145 a month |
| AI answers | No AI fee. Your own agent connects and drafts replies | $0.99 per Fin outcome, if you turn it on |
| Self-hosting | Free, open source | Not possible |
| Free trial | None | 14 days |

Intercom prices are from intercom.com/pricing on November 8, 2026. The list price is $29 a seat, new customers see $19, and startups can apply for a bigger discount. [All the prices side by side](/compare/).

For a team of three, that's $50 to $80 a month apart, depending on which Intercom price you get.

## What Intercom does that Dinghy doesn't

- Fin, a hosted AI agent that learns from your help center and answers customers on its own.
- A help center, product tours, banners and tooltips.
- Email, phone and other channels in the same inbox.
- Workflows, routing rules, advanced reporting and a big app marketplace. Dinghy has analytics, an API, webhooks and an MCP server, but no marketplace.

If those are why you're looking, Dinghy is the wrong tool.

## What Dinghy does differently

Intercom's bill grows with every seat and, if you use Fin, with every Fin outcome. Dinghy Cloud costs $7 a month for the workspace, up to 5 people, so hiring your second support person costs nothing. You pay at signup and can cancel anytime.

Dinghy has no AI of its own. If you want a bot, connect your own agent through MCP or the API. Its replies wait as drafts until a person sends them, unless you let it send directly, and you pay your model provider, not Dinghy. [Bring your own AI agent](/blog/bring-your-own-ai-agent) has more. And because it's open source, you're never stuck. Export everything to a file and import it into a [self-hosted Dinghy](/open-source-live-chat), which is free.

## Switching from Intercom

There's no importer from Intercom. The Dinghy tag goes where the Intercom snippet was. [Moving over from another chat](/compare/#moving-over-from-another-chat) has the steps.

If you signed users in with Intercom's identity verification, Dinghy has the same idea. Your server signs a short token, and operators see who they're talking to. It's in [Show who is chatting](/docs/add-to-your-site#show-who-is-chatting).
