# Dinghy vs LiveChat

LiveChat is one of the oldest names in website chat, and it's a polished product. It's also priced per seat, so the bill grows every time someone joins support. Dinghy is a smaller chat with a flat price.

## The quick answer

Pick LiveChat if you want a mature chat with a big integration marketplace, reports, routing and AI features, and per-seat pricing is fine for your budget. Pick Dinghy if you want a chat on your site and a shared inbox for a team of up to five, at $7 a month, or free on your own server.

## Price for a small team

| | Dinghy Cloud | LiveChat Essential |
|---|---|---|
| 1 person | $7 a month | $19 to $25 a month |
| 3 people | $7 a month | $57 to $75 a month |
| 5 people | $7 a month | $95 to $125 a month |
| Chat history | Text kept while you subscribe, images and files for 12 months | 60 days on Essential, unlimited from Growth at $79 to $99 a seat |
| White-label widget | No Dinghy label | Enterprise only |
| Canned responses, tags, business hours | Saved replies, labels, business hours | Yes |
| AI | None built in. Bring your own agent, no AI fee | AI features and chatbots |
| Self-hosting | Free, open source | Not possible |
| Free trial | None | 14 days, no card |

LiveChat prices are from livechat.com/pricing on November 9, 2026. The lower number is per seat billed yearly, the higher one billed monthly. [All the prices side by side](/compare/).

## What LiveChat does that Dinghy doesn't

- A large marketplace of apps and integrations.
- Detailed reports, chat routing rules, and tools for bigger support teams. Dinghy has analytics with reply times and busiest hours, and manual assignment.
- Built-in AI features and a path to chatbots.
- A white-label widget, on the Enterprise plan.

If you run a support team of ten or twenty, those things matter, and LiveChat is built for that.

## What Dinghy does differently

Dinghy Cloud costs $7 a month for the workspace, up to 5 people. Adding your third or fifth person costs nothing, and your conversation history doesn't stop at 60 days.

Dinghy has no AI of its own. Every answer comes from a person on your team, unless you connect your own agent through MCP or the API. Even then, its replies wait as drafts for a person to send by default. You pay at signup and can cancel anytime.
