# Dinghy vs LiveChat

LiveChat has been around for years. But it's per seat, so the bill goes up every time someone joins support. Dinghy is a smaller chat with one flat price.

## The quick answer

Use LiveChat if you need its integrations, reports, routing and AI, and can pay per seat. Dinghy has website chat and a shared inbox for up to five people at $7 a month. Or run it free on your own server.

## Price for a small team

| | Dinghy Cloud | LiveChat Essential |
|---|---|---|
| 1 person | $7 a month | $19 to $25 a month |
| 3 people | $7 a month | $57 to $75 a month |
| 5 people | $7 a month | $95 to $125 a month |
| Chat history | Text kept while you subscribe, images and files for 12 months | 60 days on Essential, unlimited from Growth at $79 to $99 a seat |
| White-label widget | Turn off "Powered by Dinghy" for free | Enterprise only |
| Canned responses, tags, business hours | Saved replies, labels, business hours | Yes |
| AI | None built in. Bring your own agent, no AI fee | AI features and chatbots |
| Self-hosting | Free, open source | Not possible |
| Free trial | None | 14 days, no card |

LiveChat prices are from livechat.com/pricing on November 9, 2026. The lower number is per seat billed yearly, the higher one billed monthly. [All the prices side by side](/compare/).

## What LiveChat does that Dinghy doesn't

- A marketplace of apps and integrations.
- Detailed reports and routing rules. Dinghy has analytics with reply times and busiest hours, and you assign chats by hand.
- Built-in AI features and chatbots.
- A white-label widget, on Enterprise.

For ten or twenty people, check LiveChat's reports, routing and integrations.

## What Dinghy does differently

Dinghy Cloud is $7 a month for the workspace, up to 5 people. Your third or fifth person costs nothing. And your history doesn't stop at 60 days.

Dinghy has no AI of its own. Every answer comes from someone on your team, unless you connect your own agent through MCP or the API. Even then, by default its replies wait as drafts for a person to send. You pay at signup and can cancel anytime.
