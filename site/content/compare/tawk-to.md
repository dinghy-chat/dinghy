# Dinghy vs tawk.to

tawk.to is free. It makes money from add-ons instead: removing its branding, AI answers, and hiring its chat agents by the hour. Dinghy charges a small flat price and has no add-ons at all.

## The quick answer

If $0 matters most and you don't mind "Powered by tawk.to" on your site, use tawk.to. It's a fair deal. If you'd pay the $29 a month to remove the branding anyway, Dinghy Cloud does it for $7, or Dinghy is free on your own server.

## Price

| | Dinghy Cloud | tawk.to |
|---|---|---|
| Base price | $7 a month for up to 5 people | Free |
| Remove the branding | Nothing to remove | $29 a month |
| AI answers | None built in. Bring your own agent, no AI fee | AI Assist, from $29 a month |
| Hired chat agents | None | $1 an hour |
| Self-hosting | Free, open source | Not possible |

tawk.to prices are from tawk.to/pricing on November 8, 2026. [All the prices side by side](/compare/).

## What tawk.to does that Dinghy doesn't

- A free plan with no time limit.
- A knowledge base, ticketing and more.
- Agents you can hire to answer for you.
- AI Assist, if you pay for it.

## What Dinghy does differently

Dinghy has no "powered by" label and no add-on menu. On Dinghy Cloud, $7 a month is the whole bill. If you want AI answers, connect your own agent through MCP or the API. You pay your model provider, and its replies wait as drafts for your team to send.

## Moving over

There's no importer from tawk.to. The Dinghy tag goes where the tawk.to snippet was, usually just before `</body>`. [Moving over from another chat](/compare/#moving-over-from-another-chat) has the steps.
