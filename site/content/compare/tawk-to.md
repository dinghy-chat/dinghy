# Dinghy vs tawk.to

tawk.to is free. It makes its money on add-ons: removing its branding, AI answers, and chat agents you hire by the hour. Dinghy charges one flat price and has no add-ons.

## The quick answer

If $0 matters most and you don't mind "Powered by tawk.to" on your site, use tawk.to. If you'd pay the $29 a month to remove the branding anyway, Dinghy Cloud is $7, or free on your own server.

## Price

| | Dinghy Cloud | tawk.to |
|---|---|---|
| Base price | $7 a month for up to 5 people | Free |
| Remove the branding | Free, one switch in Settings | $29 a month |
| AI answers | None built in. Bring your own agent, no AI fee | AI Assist, from $29 a month |
| Hired chat agents | None | $1 an hour |
| Self-hosting | Free, open source | Not possible |

tawk.to prices are from tawk.to/pricing on November 8, 2026. [All the prices side by side](/compare/).

## What tawk.to does that Dinghy doesn't

- A free plan with no time limit.
- A knowledge base and ticketing.
- Agents you can hire to answer for you.
- AI Assist, if you pay for it.

## What Dinghy does differently

The "Powered by Dinghy" line turns off for free, and there's no add-on menu. On Dinghy Cloud, $7 a month is the whole bill. If you want AI answers, connect your own agent through MCP or the API. You pay your model provider, and its replies wait as drafts for your team to send.

## Moving over

There's no importer from tawk.to. The Dinghy tag goes where the tawk.to snippet was, usually just before `</body>`. The steps are in [Moving over from another chat](/compare/#moving-over-from-another-chat).
