# Tidio pricing in 2026

Tidio prices by conversations a month, not by seat. The Free plan covers 50 human conversations a month. Human only Starter is $29 a month, or $290 a year, for 100 conversations and up to 10 seats. With Lyro AI, Starter is $39 a month for 50 conversations. More conversations means a higher tier.

Prices are from [Tidio's pricing page](https://www.tidio.com/pricing/) and [Tidio's plan and price list](https://help.tidio.com/articles/pricing/), checked on November 27, 2026. Annual billing on Starter and Growth is 10 times the monthly price, so you get 2 months free. Flows, Inbox AI and Voice get the same deal. Help Center and badge removal cost the same either way. Subscriptions started before November 16, 2026 keep their old plan and price.

Lyro has no separate price. Its conversations come out of the same quota as your team's, and a With Lyro AI plan costs more than Human only for the same number.

Starter and Growth allow up to 10 seats. Starter has live chat, tickets, social channels, a live visitors list and basic analytics. Growth adds advanced analytics, automatic routing, quick replies with macros, user permissions, Shopify actions and a live typing preview. Plus starts at $300 a month plus usage, through sales, with custom seats and limits. Premium is priced by sales.

New accounts get a 7-day trial of Growth with Lyro AI, no card needed. When it ends, the project owner has to pick a paid plan or select Downgrade to Free before the rest of the panel opens again, per [Tidio's billing FAQ](https://help.tidio.com/articles/billing-faq/).

## The conversation limit

A conversation counts once your team or Lyro replies in it. Messages from visitors that nobody answers don't count, and neither does spam.

When you reach your limit, the Usage and plan button in your Tidio panel says "Limit reached." Per [Tidio's article on the monthly limit](https://help.tidio.com/articles/monthly-conversations-limit/), your team can't reply to new conversations and Lyro stops answering until the quota renews or you raise it. On standard monthly plans, [automatic upgrades](https://help.tidio.com/articles/automatic-upgrades/) are on by default. At 95% of your limit, Tidio moves you to the next tier and you pay that tier's price from then on. There's no tier above 1,000 conversations a month, so past that you ask Tidio about Plus. You turn it off in Settings > Project > Billing > Subscription, one package at a time, so check the switch on monthly Flows and Voice add-ons too. Annual plans don't upgrade on their own, so you raise the limit by hand.

This is the part to plan for. A busy month either cuts off new chats or moves you to a higher price that stays until you downgrade, and a downgrade only starts at the next billing period.

## The charges people miss

**Flows.** Automated flows reach 100 visitors a month free. Above that, the Flows add-on starts at $29 a month for 2,000 visitors and goes up to $299 a month for 100,000.

**No Tidio branding.** Removing the badge is $20 a month on Growth. Plus and Premium include it.

**Inbox AI.** Priced by your plan's conversation tier, from $10 a month at 100 conversations up to $140 at 2,000. It has no usage limit of its own.

**Help Center.** $10 a month.

**Voice.** From $19 a month for 20 conversations, up to $349 a month for 1,000.

## When free chat is enough

The Free plan is enough if you answer fewer than 50 chats a month. tawk.to is free. Crisp and Chatwoot have free plans too. See [all the live chat prices side by side](/compare/).

## When Tidio is worth it

Tidio is worth it for a small store that wants an AI agent on common questions, Shopify actions in the inbox and up to 10 people on one plan. Lyro is priced into the quota, so there's no separate bill per AI answer. If your chat volume jumps around, the limit and the automatic upgrade make the bill harder to predict.

## Dinghy, if you only need website chat

Dinghy is a small open-source live chat with a shared inbox. Dinghy Cloud is $9 a month for the workspace, for up to 5 people, with no conversation limit. It's free on your own server. It has no built-in bot, no flows builder and no store actions, so if you want Lyro, stay on Tidio. You can connect your own AI agent, and its replies start as drafts for a person to check. See [Dinghy vs Tidio](/compare/tidio).

## FAQ

### How much does Tidio cost per month?

Human only Starter is $29 a month, or $290 a year, for 100 conversations. With Lyro AI, Starter is $39 a month for 50. Growth tiers go up to $199 a month Human only and $700 with Lyro, for 1,000 conversations.

### Is Tidio free?

Yes, for 50 human conversations a month. Free also gets 50 Lyro conversations once, and they don't renew.

### What happens when I hit Tidio's conversation limit?

Your team can't reply to new conversations and Lyro stops until the quota renews, unless you raise the limit. On a standard monthly plan below 1,000 conversations, automatic upgrades are on by default and move you to the next tier at 95%.

### How many seats does Tidio include?

Up to 10 on Starter and Growth. Plus has a custom number.
