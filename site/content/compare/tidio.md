# Dinghy vs Tidio

Tidio is a live chat with a strong push toward its AI agent, Lyro, and a lot of e-commerce features. Dinghy is just the chat and the inbox. Which one fits depends on whether you want a ready-made bot talking to your customers.

## The quick answer

Pick Tidio if you run a store, want an AI agent to handle common questions, and like having flows and e-commerce tools in one place. Pick Dinghy if you want people on your team to answer every chat, without a conversation count. You can still connect your own AI agent, and its replies wait as drafts for a person to send.

Tidio prices are from tidio.com/pricing on November 8, 2026. Tidio's page has a yearly billing option, so its monthly rate may be higher. [All the prices side by side](/compare/).

## The conversation count

Tidio's plans include a set number of "billable conversations" each month. On Starter, that's 100. A launch or a shipping delay can use that up, and then you're looking at an upgrade.

Dinghy doesn't count conversations, so on Dinghy Cloud a busy month costs the same $7 as a quiet one.

## What Tidio does that Dinghy doesn't

- Lyro, a hosted AI agent that answers on its own.
- Flows, which are automated messages and chatbots you build.
- E-commerce tools and many integrations, including store platforms. Dinghy has an API and webhooks instead.
- Other channels, like an email inbox and social messengers. Dinghy only emails your reply to a visitor who left.

## What Dinghy does differently

Dinghy doesn't sell a bot. A person on your team writes every reply, unless you connect your own agent through MCP or the API. Even then, its replies wait as drafts by default. See [Bring your own AI agent](/blog/bring-your-own-ai-agent). There's no count of conversations, contacts or visitors either.

And if you'd rather not pay at all, Dinghy is open source and free on your own server.
