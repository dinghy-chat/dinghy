# Zendesk pricing in 2026

Zendesk charges per agent, per month. Support Team is $19 an agent on yearly billing and $25 on monthly billing. Live chat on your website starts with Suite Team, at $55 an agent billed yearly or $69 billed monthly. Five agents on Suite Team pay $275 to $345 a month before any AI or add-ons.

All prices below are from [Zendesk's pricing page](https://www.zendesk.com/pricing/), checked on November 27, 2026.

## Every plan

| Plan | Billed yearly | Billed monthly | What it adds |
|---|---|---|---|
| Support Team | $19 per agent a month | $25 per agent a month | Email and ticketing, routing, prebuilt dashboards, saved responses, automations and triggers |
| Suite Team | $55 per agent a month | $69 per agent a month | Messaging and live chat, AI agents, an AI knowledge base, omnichannel routing, phone |
| Suite Professional | $115 per agent a month | $149 per agent a month | Admin Copilot, AI writing tools with 5 uses per agent a month shared across the team, skills-based routing, IVR phone menus, up to 5 help centers |
| Suite Enterprise | Talk to sales | Talk to sales | Intelligent triage, approval workflows, a sandbox, custom agent roles |

The pricing page lists messaging and live chat from Suite Team up. If chat on your site is the reason you're looking, Support Team is not the plan to price.

Suite Team comes with 1 help center and 2 departments. Suite Professional raises that to up to 5 help centers and unlimited departments.

The free trial lasts 14 days and gives you Suite Professional, Copilot included.

## The charges people miss

**AI resolutions.** Zendesk's AI agents are included on Suite plans, but the resolutions are metered. On the older billing model, Suite Team includes 5 automated resolutions per agent a month and Suite Professional includes 10. Past that, a resolution costs $1.50 if you commit to a number in advance, or $2.00 pay as you go. A five-agent Suite Team gets 25 a month. If the bot resolves 300 chats, the other 275 cost $412.50 to $550 on that model.

If you bought or renewed Suite after May 18, 2026 and use Zendesk AI, you're on the [newer model](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/11504709759898-How-can-I-tell-which-pricing-model-I-have). It gives you a dollar allowance instead of a count, $2 per agent a month on Suite Team and $5 on Suite Professional, per [Zendesk's resolution allowance article](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/10479587390106-Managing-resolution-allowances). Each resolution then costs a different amount depending on its tier, per [Zendesk's article on resolution tiers](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/9570369117338). You can also tell Zendesk to pause the AI agent when the allowance runs out, so a busy month can't run up overage.

**Copilot.** The AI assistant for agents is $50 per agent a month on yearly billing. That's almost the price of another Suite Team seat.

**Other add-ons.** The Workforce Engagement bundle is $50 per agent a month billed yearly, and Contact Center is $83. Quality assurance, extra storage, phone numbers and call minutes are add-ons too. The pricing page marks them as add-ons and doesn't print a price.

Per month. The lower number is yearly billing, the higher one monthly billing. AI resolutions past the allowance and add-ons come on top.

## Cheaper options for email or chat

Most small teams look at Zendesk for one of two jobs.

If you need email tickets, Support Team at $19 to $25 an agent does that without the Suite. The public help center comes with Suite plans, though. For email tickets and a help center at a low per-seat price, [Freshdesk Growth](/compare/freshdesk-pricing) includes a knowledge base at $19 an agent, and [Help Scout](/compare/help-scout-pricing) includes a docs site even on its free plan.

If you need chat on your website, you don't need a help desk. tawk.to is free. Crisp and Chatwoot have free plans. See [all the live chat prices side by side](/compare/).

## When Zendesk is worth it

Zendesk earns its price when you have email, phone, chat and social in one queue, with SLAs, routing by skill and reports someone reads every week. It also fits when you expect to grow past ten agents and don't want to switch tools later. For two people answering website chat, most of what you pay for goes unused.

## Dinghy, if you only need website chat

Dinghy is a small open-source live chat with a shared inbox. Dinghy Cloud is $9 a month for the workspace, for up to 5 people, with no per-agent price and no AI billing. You can also run it free on your own server. It has no tickets, no email inbox, no phone and no built-in bot, so it does not replace Zendesk for a team that uses those. You can connect your own AI agent, and its replies start as drafts for a person to check. See [Dinghy vs Zendesk](/compare/zendesk).

## FAQ

### How much is Zendesk per month?

Support Team is $19 per agent a month on yearly billing and $25 on monthly billing. Suite Team, the first plan with live chat, is $55 or $69. Suite Professional is $115 or $149. Enterprise prices come from sales.

### Does Zendesk have a free plan?

No. There is a 14-day free trial of Suite Professional.

### How much do Zendesk AI agents cost?

If you bought or renewed Suite after May 18, 2026 and use Zendesk AI, Suite Team gives you $2 of resolution credit per agent a month and Suite Professional $5, and the price of each resolution depends on its tier. Older accounts get 5 or 10 resolutions per agent, then pay $1.50 each on a commitment or $2.00 pay as you go. Copilot for agents is $50 per agent a month billed yearly.

### Is Zendesk Suite Team enough for a small team?

For chat, email and a help center with one brand, yes. You get 1 help center and 2 departments. Skills-based routing and more help centers need Suite Professional.
