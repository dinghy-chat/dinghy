# Zendesk vs Freshdesk for a small team

Both are full help desks priced per agent. For a small team that wants email and website chat, Freshdesk costs less. Zendesk Suite Team is $55 an agent a month billed yearly. Freshdesk Omni Growth is $29 an agent a month billed annually. Zendesk has more tools for larger teams. For one to five agents, most of them don't matter yet.

## Price for 1, 3 and 5 agents

With email and website chat, on the cheapest plan that has both:

| | Zendesk Suite Team | Freshdesk Omni Growth |
|---|---|---|
| 1 agent | $55 to $69 a month | $29 a month |
| 3 agents | $165 to $207 a month | $87 a month |
| 5 agents | $275 to $345 a month | $145 a month |
| Free trial | 14 days | 14 days |

Zendesk's lower number is billed yearly and the higher one monthly, from [Zendesk's pricing page](https://www.zendesk.com/pricing/). Freshdesk Omni prices are billed annually, from [Freshdesk Omni's pricing page](https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/omni/pricing/). More detail on the [Zendesk pricing page](/compare/zendesk-pricing) and the [Freshdesk pricing page](/compare/freshdesk-pricing).

If you only need email tickets, both start at $19 an agent a month billed yearly. On Zendesk that's Support Team, $25 billed monthly. On Freshdesk it's [Freshdesk Growth](https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/pricing/), which Freshworks describes as service over email. Freshdesk also has a [Free Program](https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/50000010099-explore-freshdesk-free-plan) for up to 2 agents, free for 6 months.

## Chat

Zendesk lists messaging and live chat from Suite Team. Support Team, the $19 plan, doesn't list it.

Freshworks splits chat across products. Plain Freshdesk is email. Freshdesk Omni adds chat and other channels in the same inbox. [Freshchat](https://www.freshworks.com/live-chat-software/pricing/) is chat on its own, with a free plan for up to 10 agents and paid plans from $19 an agent a month billed annually. A small team that wants email in Freshdesk and chat in Freshchat can pay $19 an agent for email and nothing for chat, but then the two live in separate products.

## Email

Both handle email tickets on every plan, with shared views, assignment and rules. Email is the core of both products.

## AI and what it costs

Both sell a bot that answers customers by itself, and both bill it by use.

| | Zendesk | Freshdesk |
|---|---|---|
| Bot answers included | $2 of resolution credit per agent a month on Suite Team, per the [allowance article](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/10479587390106-Managing-resolution-allowances). Older accounts get 5 resolutions per agent | 500 Freddy AI Agent sessions, once per account |
| After that | A price per resolution that depends on its tier. On the older model, $1.50 committed or $2.00 pay as you go | $49 per 100 sessions |
| Help for agents | Copilot, $50 an agent a month billed yearly | Freddy AI Copilot, $29 an agent a month billed annually, on Pro and Enterprise |

Zendesk counts resolutions. Freshdesk counts sessions, and a chat session lasts 24 hours from when it starts. With three agents on Zendesk Suite Team, you get $6 of credit a month, or 15 resolutions on the older model, before you pay per answer. You're on the newer model if you [bought or renewed Suite after May 18, 2026](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/11504709759898-How-can-I-tell-which-pricing-model-I-have) and use Zendesk AI. Freshdesk's 500 sessions are a one-time allowance, not a monthly one.

## Setup

Both are big products. Expect to connect your support address, set up groups and views, write a few rules and macros, and add the widget to your site. Neither is a ten-minute job if you want it to work well. Both give you 14 days to try it before you pay.

## Who each one suits

Pick Zendesk if you expect to grow into a larger support team, want Zendesk's app marketplace, or need its routing, SLAs and reports later.

Pick Freshdesk if price per agent matters, most of your support is email, or you already use other Freshworks products. Freshchat's free plan is worth a look if chat is a small part of your support.

## If you only need website chat

Both are help desks first. If your team is one to five people answering chats on your own website, you don't need tickets, SLAs or a phone line. Dinghy is a smaller option at $9 a month for the workspace, or free on your own server. See [Dinghy vs Zendesk](/compare/zendesk) and [Dinghy vs Freshdesk](/compare/freshdesk).
