# Dinghy vs Zendesk

Zendesk is a full help desk for big support teams. Live chat and messaging on your website start with its Suite plans, priced per agent. Dinghy is a small website chat with a flat price.

## The quick answer

Pick Zendesk if you need tickets, email, phone, a help center, AI agents and detailed reporting in one system, and you have the team and budget to run it. Pick Dinghy if you're one to five people and you want visitors on your site to reach you.

## Price for a small team

| | Dinghy Cloud | Zendesk Suite Team |
|---|---|---|
| 1 person | $7 a month | $55 to $69 a month |
| 3 people | $7 a month | $165 to $207 a month |
| 5 people | $7 a month | $275 to $345 a month |
| AI | None built in. Bring your own agent, no AI fee | 5 automated resolutions per agent a month, then $1.50 to $2 each |
| Self-hosting | Free, open source | Not possible |
| Free trial | None | 14 days |

Zendesk prices are from zendesk.com/pricing on November 9, 2026. The lower number is per agent billed yearly, the higher one billed monthly. Zendesk's cheaper Support Team plan at $19 to $25 an agent has the web widget, but its page lists messaging and live chat from Suite Team. [All the prices side by side](/compare/).

## What Zendesk does that Dinghy doesn't

- Tickets, email, phone, social channels and a help center in one place.
- Built-in AI agents that answer and resolve requests on their own.
- Routing, SLAs and deep reporting. Dinghy has saved replies, labels, analytics and business hours, but no routing rules or SLAs.
- A huge marketplace of integrations. Dinghy has an API, webhooks and an MCP server instead.

If you run a support department, Zendesk is built for you and Dinghy isn't.

## What Dinghy does differently

Dinghy Cloud is $7 a month for the workspace, up to 5 people, with no per-agent price and no AI billing. You pay at signup and can cancel anytime. Most setup is the widget's look in Settings > Appearance and your team. If you want AI answers, connect your own agent. Its replies wait as drafts for a person to send.
