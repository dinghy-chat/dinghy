# Dinghy vs Zendesk

Zendesk does tickets, email, phone and website chat. Live chat and messaging on your site start with the Suite plans, priced per agent. Dinghy is a small website chat at one flat price.

## The quick answer

Use Zendesk if you need tickets, email, phone, a help center, AI agents and detailed reports. Use Dinghy for website chat with one to five people.

## Price for a small team

| | Dinghy Cloud | Zendesk Suite Team |
|---|---|---|
| 1 person | $7 a month | $55 to $69 a month |
| 3 people | $7 a month | $165 to $207 a month |
| 5 people | $7 a month | $275 to $345 a month |
| AI | None built in. Bring your own agent, no AI fee | 5 automated resolutions per agent a month, then $1.50 to $2 each |
| Self-hosting | Free, open source | Not possible |
| Free trial | None | 14 days |

Zendesk prices are from zendesk.com/pricing on November 9, 2026. The lower number is per agent billed yearly, the higher one billed monthly. The cheaper Support Team plan, $19 to $25 an agent, has the web widget, but the page only lists messaging and live chat from Suite Team. [All the prices side by side](/compare/).

## What Zendesk does that Dinghy doesn't

- Tickets, email, phone, social channels and a help center together.
- Built-in AI agents that answer and resolve requests by themselves.
- Routing, SLAs and detailed reports. Dinghy has saved replies, labels, analytics and business hours, but no routing rules and no SLAs.
- A marketplace of integrations. Dinghy has an API, webhooks and an MCP server instead.

## What Dinghy does differently

Dinghy Cloud is $7 a month for the workspace, up to 5 people. No per-agent price, no AI billing. You pay at signup and can cancel anytime. Change the widget in Settings > Appearance, then invite your team. If you want AI answers, connect your own agent. Its replies wait as drafts for a person to send.
