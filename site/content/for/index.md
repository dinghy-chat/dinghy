# Who uses Dinghy, and how

You get a chat button on your site and a shared inbox for up to 5 people. On Dinghy Cloud that's $7 a month. Or you run it yourself on one small server, for free.

## What every team gets

The tag loads a script under 4 KB, and the full chat loads when a visitor opens it, or comes back to a chat they started. Visitors and your team can both send images and files. Your team answers from an operator app that installs on a phone, with push alerts, saved replies, notes, labels and analytics. Dinghy has no AI of its own. But your own agent can answer through MCP or the API, and by default its replies wait as drafts until a person sends them. There are no campaigns and no help center.

If you need those, the [comparisons](/compare/) tell you which tool to look at instead.
