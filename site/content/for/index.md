# Who uses Dinghy, and how

Dinghy is a live chat for small teams who want people on their site to reach a real person. It's a chat button on your site and a shared inbox for up to 5 people on Dinghy Cloud, at $7 a month. Or you run it yourself, free, on one small server.

It does one job, so it fits some teams well and others not at all. Each page below covers one kind of team, how to set it up, and when you should pick something else.

## What every team gets

Every team gets the same product. The tag loads a script under 4 KB, and the full chat loads only when a visitor opens it. Visitors and your team can both send images and files. Your team answers from an operator app that installs on a phone, with push alerts, saved replies, notes, labels and analytics. Dinghy has no AI of its own, but your own agent can answer through MCP or the API, with its replies waiting as drafts for a person by default. There are no campaigns and no help center.

If you need those, the [comparisons](/compare/) say which tool to look at instead.
