# Live chat for solo founders

If you run the business alone, you're the support team. Dinghy gives you a chat on your site and an inbox on your phone, with a push alert when someone writes. There's no built-in bot, so every reply is yours. Unless you connect your own AI agent, and even then you approve its drafts by default. Dinghy Cloud costs $7 a month. Self-hosting is free.

## Your inbox is on your phone

The operator app is a web app you can add to your home screen. It sends a push notification when a visitor writes, and you answer right there, with text, an image or a file.

1. Sign up, and add the tag to your site. The [install guides](/guides/) cover WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Next.js and more, or let [Claude Code](/guides/claude-code) add it for you.
2. Open your Dinghy address on your phone. On an iPhone, open it in Safari, select Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open Dinghy from the Home Screen.
3. Go to Settings > Notifications, select Turn on, then Send a test.
4. Turn it on on your laptop too. Each device has its own setting.

No passwords. You sign in with a link sent to your email, and you can add a code from an authenticator app as a second step. Phones sometimes delay or drop notifications. Check the inbox on days you expect questions. The [operator guide](/docs/operator-guide#get-alerts) has the details.

## When you're asleep

A visitor who writes while you're offline can leave an email in the chat. If they've left the page when you reply, the answer goes to their inbox. They can answer from their mail app, and it lands in the same conversation. On Dinghy Cloud there's nothing to set up. Self-hosted, you need a mail server, plus an inbound address for [replies by email](/docs/self-hosting#replies-by-email).

So a message at 2 a.m. doesn't get lost. You answer over coffee, they get it by email. Set your hours in Settings > Business hours. Outside them, the chat tells visitors when you're back and asks for their email. Save the answers you give every day as saved replies, and type **/** to insert one.

## No bot pretending to be you

Dinghy has no AI of its own and charges no AI fee. If a visitor gets an answer, you wrote it or you approved it. People who write to a one-person company often want to reach you. A bot answering in your voice without you would mislead them.

If you want help, connect your own agent, like Claude Code, through MCP. Its replies wait under the conversation as drafts, and you send, edit or discard each one. When a bot replies, the visitor sees its name and a bot icon. [Live chat for AI agents](/for/ai-agents) has the setup, and [Bring your own AI agent](/blog/bring-your-own-ai-agent) explains why I built it this way.

## What it costs

Dinghy Cloud is $7 a month per workspace, flat. If you hire help later, up to 5 people fit in the same $7. You pay at signup and can cancel anytime. There's no free plan on the cloud.

Self-host it if you want the data on your own machine or don't want to pay $7 a month. Same product, every feature, on one small Linux server. The [self-hosting guide](/docs/self-hosting) has the one install script.

## When Dinghy isn't the right fit

- You want to pay nothing and don't want to run a server. Some tools have free plans with their branding on the chat. [See the prices](/compare/).
- You can't check messages for days at a time. A contact form sets better expectations than a chat button.
- You want a ready-made bot that answers first with no setup, or a help center for common questions. Dinghy has neither. It only works with an agent you bring.
