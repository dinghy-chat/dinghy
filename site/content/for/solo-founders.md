# Live chat for solo founders

If you run the business alone, you are the support team. Dinghy gives you a chat on your site and an inbox on your phone, with push alerts when someone writes. Dinghy has no bot of its own, so every reply is yours unless you connect your own AI agent, and even then you approve its drafts by default. Dinghy Cloud costs $7 a month. Self-hosting is free.

## Your inbox is on your phone

The operator app is a web app you can add to your home screen. It sends a push notification when a visitor writes, and you answer right there, with text, an image or a file.

1. Sign up, and add the tag to your site. The [install guides](/guides/) cover WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Next.js and more, or let [Claude Code](/guides/claude-code) add it for you.
2. Open your Dinghy address on your phone. On an iPhone, open it in Safari, select Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open Dinghy from the Home Screen.
3. Go to Settings > Notifications, select Turn on, then Send a test.
4. Turn it on on your laptop too. Each device has its own setting.

There are no passwords. You sign in with a link sent to your email, and you can add a code from an authenticator app as a second step. Phones sometimes delay or drop notifications, so check the inbox now and then on a day you expect questions. The [operator guide](/docs/operator-guide#get-alerts) has the details.

## When you're asleep

A visitor who writes while you're offline can leave an email in the chat. When you reply, the answer goes to their inbox if they've left the page. They can answer from their mail app, and it lands in the same conversation. On Dinghy Cloud this works as is. On a self-hosted install, it needs a mail server.

So a message at 2 a.m. doesn't vanish. You answer over coffee, and they get it by email. Set your hours in Settings > Business hours, and the chat tells visitors when you're back and asks for their email outside those hours. Save the answers you give every day as saved replies, and type **/** to insert one.

## No bot pretending to be you

Dinghy has no AI of its own and charges no AI fee. If a visitor gets an answer, you wrote it or you approved it. Someone who writes to a one-person company is often hoping to reach you, and a bot that answers in your voice without you would mislead them.

If you want help, connect your own agent, like Claude Code, through MCP. Its replies wait under the conversation as drafts, and you send, edit or discard each one. Replies from a bot show its name and a bot icon to the visitor. [Live chat for AI agents](/for/ai-agents) has the setup, and [Bring your own AI agent](/blog/bring-your-own-ai-agent) explains why it works this way.

## What it costs

Dinghy Cloud is $7 a month per workspace, flat. If you hire help later, up to 5 people fit in the same $7. Payment is at signup, and you can cancel anytime. There's no free plan on the cloud.

If $7 a month matters, or you want the data on your own machine, self-host it. It's the same product, with every feature, on one small Linux server. The [self-hosting guide](/docs/self-hosting) has the one install script.

## When Dinghy isn't the right fit

- You want to pay nothing and don't want to run a server. Some tools have free plans with their branding on the chat. [See the prices](/compare/).
- You can't check messages for days at a time. A contact form sets better expectations than a chat button.
- You want a ready-made bot that answers first with no setup, or a help center for common questions. Dinghy has neither. It works only with an agent you bring.
