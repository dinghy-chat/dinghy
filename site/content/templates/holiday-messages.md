# Holiday messages for customers

A holiday message has to answer two questions. When are you closed, and when will someone reply? Put both in the first line, with real dates. The season greeting is optional, and short is better. Below are templates for a holiday hours notice, a chat away message, a support out of office email and a few greetings that don't read like a greeting card.

![A sailboat at anchor next to a small island with a palm tree and a hammock, under a crescent moon](/img/illustrations/holiday-messages.webp)

## The placeholders

Fill in the words in curly brackets before you send:

- `{company}` is your company or product name.
- `{holiday}` is the holiday, or "the holidays" if you'd rather not name one.
- `{closed from}` and `{back on}` are real dates with the day, like "Thursday, December 24" and "Monday, January 4".
- `{hours}` are your opening hours with the time zone, like "9am to 5pm Eastern".
- `{help link}` is where people can find answers on their own, like your docs or FAQ.
- `{urgent contact}` is how to reach someone in an emergency, if you offer that.
- `{name}` is the customer's first name, and `{your name}` is yours.
- `{teammate}` is who covers for you, and `{response time}` is how long a reply takes while you're short-staffed, like "two days".

Other brackets, like `{opening time}` or `{reduced hours start}`, name what goes in them.

## Holiday hours notices

Use these on your site, in a banner, in an email to customers or on social media, a week or so before you close. Name the dates, not "the holiday period". If you're open with fewer people, say replies will be slower instead of pretending nothing changed.

```template
We're closed for {holiday} from {closed from}, and we're back on {back on}. Messages sent while we're away will get a reply when we're back, in the order they came in.
```

```template
From {reduced hours start}, we're open {hours} with a smaller team, so replies may take a bit longer. Normal hours are back on {back on}.
```

```template
Heads up, our support team is off from {closed from} and back on {back on}. Orders still ship as normal. If you need anything before then, write to us this week.
```

```template
Subject: Our holiday hours

Hi {name},

A quick note before the holidays. We're closed from {closed from} and back on {back on}.

You can still use {company} as normal while we're away. If something comes up, write to us and we'll reply when we're back. For most questions, {help link} has the answer.

Thanks for a good year.

The {company} team
```

## Chat away messages

Use these in your chat widget while nobody's online. Keep them to one or two sentences, because the widget is small and people skim. Say when you're back, and ask for an email so the reply can find them after they close the tab.

```template
We're off for {holiday} and back on {back on}. Leave a message and your email, and we'll reply then.
```

```template
Nobody's here right now. We're back on {back on}. Leave your question and your email address, and we'll write back.
```

```template
We're away until {back on}. Leave your message and your email, and we'll pick it up first thing when we're back.
```

```template
The team's offline for the holidays until {back on}. Leave a message and your email, and we'll get back to you then. In the meantime, {help link} has answers to most questions.
```

```template
We're closed today and back tomorrow at {opening time}. Leave a note and your email, and we'll reply first thing.
```

```template
It's a holiday here, so we're slower than usual. Someone checks in once a day, so expect a reply within 24 hours. Leave your email so it can find you.
```

## Out of office for support

Use these as the auto-reply on your support inbox. The customer reads it right after they wrote to you, often stressed. Give them the date, a place to find answers, and an emergency route if you have one. Don't make them read a paragraph about how much you value them.

```template
Subject: Got your message, we're back on {back on}

Hi,

Thanks for writing. Our support team is off for {holiday} until {back on}, and we'll reply then, in the order messages came in.

In the meantime, {help link} has answers to the most common questions.

The {company} team
```

```template
Subject: We're away until {back on}

Hi,

We got your message. We're closed until {back on}, and you'll hear back then.

If {company} is down or you can't get into your account, write to {urgent contact}. Someone checks it every day.

The {company} team
```

```template
Subject: Out of office until {back on}

Hi,

I'm away until {back on} and not checking email. For anything urgent, my teammate {teammate} can help at {urgent contact}. Otherwise, I'll reply when I'm back.

{your name}
```

```template
Subject: Running a small team over the holidays

Hi,

Thanks for your message. Most of the team is off until {back on}, and one person is covering support. Replies may take up to {response time}.

If you've already written, there's no need to send it again. You're in the queue.

The {company} team
```

## Holiday greetings that aren't cringe

Use these if you want to say something warm at the end of the year. Keep it to a few lines, say something true about your year or your customers, and don't attach a discount code unless you meant to send a sales email. Skip the puns.

```template
Thanks for sticking with us this year. We're taking a few days off, and we'll be back on {back on}. Have a good break.
```

```template
Happy holidays from the {company} team. A lot of what we built this year came from your messages, so thank you for writing them.
```

```template
That's a wrap on the year for us. We're off from {closed from} and back on {back on}. See you then.
```

```template
Subject: Thanks for this year

Hi {name},

Before we close for the holidays, a quick thank you. {one true thing about the year, like "You sent us 400 messages, and a lot of them turned into features."}

We're off from {closed from} and back on {back on}. Have a good rest of the year.

{your name}
```

```template
Season's greetings from all of us at {company}. We hope you get some time off too.
```

## The day you're back

Use these on your first day back, for messages that waited. Say sorry for the wait once, then answer. Don't open with a paragraph about your holiday.

```template
Hi {name}, thanks for waiting over the holidays. Here's the answer to your question:
```

```template
Happy new year, and sorry for the wait. I've read your message, and here's where things stand:
```

```template
Hi {name}, we're back and working through messages in order. Yours is next, and I'll have a proper answer for you by {time}.
```

## Holiday hours in Dinghy

Dinghy has [business hours](/docs/operator-guide#business-hours) in Settings > Business hours. Turn on **Use business hours**, pick a time zone and give each day up to 3 time ranges. A day with no range counts as closed. Outside those hours, the chat's header shows your away message. When the schedule has a next opening time, it also shows when you're back, like "We're back Monday at 9:00 AM", on the visitor's own clock. If your server sends mail, a new visitor gives their email before their first message, so your reply can reach them. Visitors who've chatted before, or who your site signs in, write as usual.

These hours repeat every week. So after your last shift before the break, clear the ranges for the days you're closed. If you're off for a whole week or more, that leaves no opening time to show, so the away message has to carry the date. Set it under Settings > Appearance. The header gives it one line, so keep it short and put the date first, like "Back on {back on}. Leave your email and we'll reply then." Put your usual hours and message back when you return. Pages of your site that open after a save pick up the change within 5 minutes.

## Questions people ask

### What should a holiday message for customers say?

When you're closed, when you're back and when they'll get a reply, with real dates. Add where to find answers in the meantime, and an urgent contact if you have one. A short thank you is optional.

### How do I write an out of office message for customer support?

Thank them for writing, say when the team is back, and say they'll get a reply then. Link to your help pages, and give an urgent contact if you have one. Keep it under five lines.

### What's a good away message for live chat?

One or two sentences. Say nobody's online, say when you're back, and ask for their email so you can reply after they leave. "We're back on Monday at 9. Leave your email and we'll reply then."

### When should I tell customers about holiday hours?

A week or two before you close, and again on the last working day. Also put it on the chat widget and the support inbox auto-reply, because that's where people look when they need you.

### Should a business send holiday greetings to customers?

Only if you have something real to say, like a thank you for a specific thing. A short note from a person reads better than a designed card from a brand. If you'd rather not, a clear holiday hours notice is enough.
