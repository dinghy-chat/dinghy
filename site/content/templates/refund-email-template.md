# Refund email templates

Start with the decision. If the money's on its way, say how much, where it's going and when it'll show up, all in the first two lines. If it's a no or a not yet, say why and what happens next. Below are templates for a full refund, a partial one, a refusal and a delay, each with a subject line you can keep.

![An envelope with a coin inside floats on the water like a paper boat, next to a small sailboat](/img/illustrations/refund-email.png)

## The placeholders

Fill in the words in curly brackets before you send:

- `{name}` is the customer's first name.
- `{your name}` is yours.
- `{order number}` is the order or invoice number they'll recognize.
- `{amount}` is the exact refund with its currency, like "$49.00".
- `{original amount}` is what they paid.
- `{payment method}` is where the money goes, like "the Visa ending in 4242" or "your PayPal account".
- `{days}` is the number of business days your payment provider gives for a refund to arrive, like "5 to 10". Use their number, not a guess.
- `{date}` is a real date, like "Monday, December 7".
- `{reason}` is the real reason in plain words.

## What to say when you send a refund

- The amount, exactly. "$49.00", not "your refund".
- Where it goes. Their card, PayPal, store credit.
- When they'll see it. If the bank sets the time, say so, so they don't blame you on day 4.
- What happens to the order or the account. Do they keep access, return the item, lose the subscription?
- One way to reply. Make it the same address, so they don't have to look for another.

Leave out "per our policy" unless you also explain the policy. And don't make them say thank you for their own money. A plain "Done, here are the details" is warmer than three lines of gratitude.

## Refund approved

Use these when you're giving the full amount back. Send it once your payment provider shows the refund as sent, not when the money lands. If it's still pending, say so, and use your provider's estimate for the timing line.

```template
Subject: Your refund for order {order number} is on its way

Hi {name},

I've refunded {amount} to {payment method}. It usually takes {days} business days to show up, depending on your bank.

You don't need to do anything else. If it hasn't arrived by {date}, reply to this email and I'll chase it.

{your name}
```

```template
Subject: Refund done: {amount}

Hi {name},

Done. I've refunded the full {amount} to {payment method}, and it should show up in {days} business days. I've also cancelled your {product} subscription, so you won't be charged again.

You keep access until {date}. After that, your account switches off, and your data stays for {retention period} in case you come back.

Sorry it didn't work out. If you have a minute, I'd love to know what we could have done better. No pressure.

{your name}
```

```template
Subject: Refund for the double charge on {date}

Hi {name},

You were charged twice on {date}. That was our mistake, and I'm sorry.

I've refunded the second charge of {amount} to {payment method}. It should show up in {days} business days, depending on your bank.

{your name}
```

## Partial refund

Use these when you're giving some of the money back. Show the math, so the customer doesn't have to work it out or wonder if you shorted them. If the partial amount is a choice you made, say why in one sentence.

```template
Subject: Partial refund for order {order number}

Hi {name},

I've refunded {amount} of the {original amount} you paid, to {payment method}.

Here's how I got there:
- {refunded item}: {amount for this item} back
- {kept item}: {kept amount} kept, because {reason}

It should show up in {days} business days.

If the numbers look wrong to you, reply and tell me. I'd rather fix it than have you feel short-changed.

{your name}
```

```template
Subject: Refund for the unused part of your plan

Hi {name},

You were {time used} into your yearly plan when you cancelled, so I've refunded the {months left} months you didn't use. That's {amount}, back to {payment method}.

It should arrive in {days} business days. Your account stays open until {date}.

{your name}
```

```template
Subject: About your refund for order {order number}

Hi {name},

I've refunded {amount} to {payment method}, and it should show up in {days} business days. That's the price minus {deduction}, because the item came back {condition}.

I know that's not what you hoped for. If you sent it back in better shape than that, reply and tell me, and I'll take another look at it. Photos from before you shipped it help, if you have any.

{your name}
```

## Refund denied

Use these when the answer is no. Say no in the first lines, give the real reason, and offer something if you can. Don't hide the no at the bottom. Before you turn down a late request, check when the return window starts for this order. Some start at delivery, and in the EU most goods bought online get [14 days from delivery](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/consumers/shopping/returns/index_en.htm). If you're on the fence, it's often cheaper to refund than to lose the customer and get a bad review.

```template
Subject: About your refund request for order {order number}

Hi {name},

I'm sorry, but I can't refund this one. {reason}.

What I can do is {alternative}. Would that help?

If I've misread the situation, reply and tell me what I missed. I'll look again.

{your name}
```

```template
Subject: Your refund request

Hi {name},

Thanks for asking, and I understand why. Our refund window for this order ended on {date}, so I can't refund it.

I can give you {store credit amount} in store credit instead, {how long it lasts, like "good for a year"}. Want me to set that up?

{your name}
```

```template
Subject: Re: refund for {product}

Hi {name},

I can't refund the {plan} plan, because you've used it for {time used}, and our refunds cover the first {number} days.

I can switch you to the {cheaper plan} plan from today, so your next bill is lower. Or I can cancel now so you aren't charged again. Tell me which you'd like.

{your name}
```

## Refund delayed

Use these when you've said yes but the money is late, or you need something from the customer first. Write before they ask. A refund email they didn't have to chase does a lot for their trust in you.

```template
Subject: Your refund is approved, and here's the timing

Hi {name},

Your refund of {amount} is approved. We'll send it as soon as the return reaches our warehouse. Your return tracking shows when it gets there, and I'll email you the day the refund goes out.

{your name}
```

```template
Subject: Your refund is taking longer than it should

Hi {name},

I said your refund would arrive by {promised date}, and it hasn't. Sorry about that.

I checked on it. {what the payment provider says, and its new estimate}.

If it's still not there by {follow-up date}, reply here and I'll chase our payment provider myself.

{your name}
```

```template
Subject: One thing I need for your refund

Hi {name},

I'm ready to refund {amount}, and I need one thing first. {what you need}.

As soon as I have it, I'll send the refund the same day.

{your name}
```

## Questions people ask

### How do you write a refund email?

Start with the decision. If you're sending money back, put the amount, where it's going and when it'll arrive in the first two lines. If it's a no or a not yet, say why and what happens next. Keep it short.

### What should the subject line of a refund email say?

Say the outcome, so the customer knows before they open it. "Your refund for order 1234 is on its way" works. "Regarding your request" makes them anxious.

### How do you politely say no to a refund?

Say no in the first lines, give the real reason in plain words, and offer what you can, like store credit or a cheaper plan. Invite them to reply if you've misread the case.

### How long does a refund take to show up?

It depends on the payment provider and the bank. Give the customer the estimate your provider gives, plus a date to write back if the money hasn't arrived.

### Should you explain why a refund is partial?

Yes. Show the math line by line, so they can see where the difference went. A partial refund with no breakdown gets a reply asking for one.
