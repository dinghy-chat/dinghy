Privacy policy

What data Dinghy Cloud keeps, why, and for how long. Last updated on 10 November 2026.

This policy covers Dinghy Cloud, the hosted service at app.dinghy.chat, and this website. OnceByte, LLC, a Delaware company, runs both. If you run Dinghy on your own server, the data stays on that server and we never receive it.

Two kinds of data

  • Your account. When you sign up and pay, we decide how this data is used. This policy covers it fully.
  • Your visitors' chats. When people on your website chat with you, we store their messages for you, and you decide how they are used. We follow your instructions and use the data only to run the service. Your own privacy policy must tell your visitors about the chat.

What we collect

  • For operators: name, email address, a hash of the password, workspace settings and the operator's replies.
  • For billing: Stripe holds the card and the billing address. We keep the Stripe customer ID and the subscription state.
  • For visitors: the messages and images they send, and a name or email address if they give one.
  • For every request: the IP address, which the server uses to stop abuse. It also appears in short-term server logs.

We use no advertising, no tracking cookies and no analytics tools. The chat widget stores an ID in the visitor's browser so that the conversation continues on the next page.

How we use it

  • To run the service: deliver messages, send alerts and sign-in links, and take payments.
  • To keep it safe: stop abuse and fix errors.
  • To tell you about your account, such as a failed payment or a change to these terms.

We do not sell data, and we do not use your chats to train AI models.

How long we keep it

  • Messages stay while your subscription is active. Images stay for 12 months.
  • When a subscription ends, the workspace is read-only for 30 days, then we delete it. Backup copies disappear within the next 30 days.
  • We keep billing records as long as tax law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data. If you live in the EU or the UK, the GDPR gives you these rights, and you can complain to your data protection authority. Visitors who chatted on a website that uses Dinghy should ask that website first, because it controls their chat. Email support@dinghy.chat and we answer within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we email account owners before the change takes effect.
