How to get help with Dinghy and the Dinghy Chat app.

Contact us

Email hello@dinghy.chat.

Read the docs

The docs explain how to add the chat to your website, invite your team and host Dinghy yourself.

Sign in to the app

  1. Open Dinghy Chat and enter the email address of your Dinghy account.
  2. If your team uses Dinghy Cloud, leave Self-hosted off. If your team runs its own Dinghy server, turn on Self-hosted and enter the server address, such as https://chat.example.com.
  3. Enter the code that we email you. The app needs no password.

You need an account first. Sign up at app.dinghy.chat, or ask the owner of your workspace to invite you.

Delete your account

In the app, open Settings and tap Delete my account. On the web, sign in at app.dinghy.chat, open your profile and choose Delete my account.
