Dinghy Cloud gives you a live chat widget for your website and an inbox where your team answers it. We may change features over time. If we remove a feature you pay for, we tell you by email first.

Price and payment

  • Dinghy Cloud costs $7 a month per workspace. Stripe takes the payment. We never see your full card number.

When a payment fails or you cancel

  1. If a payment fails, Stripe tries again for 7 days. The workspace keeps working during that time, and we email you.
  2. When the subscription ends, the workspace becomes read-only for 90 days. Your team can still read the conversations and download an export, and you can subscribe again to keep everything.
  3. After those 90 days, we delete the workspace and its data. We email you 30 days and 7 days before.
